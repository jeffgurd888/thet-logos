# The Vacuum Portrait Exhibit

**What this is.** Six portraits of the quantum vacuum of a U(1) lattice
gauge theory, drawn by sampling *the same measure the corpus proves
things about* — the finite Gibbs measure of Pitch 4, Stage 0/1a of the
thet-logos program, instantiated for the circle group with its
fundamental representation on a 6⁴ periodic torus. Every pixel is
traceable to a definition in the Lean corpus; every number printed on
the figures is regenerated by `python3 run_all.py` (fixed seed
20261018; total sampling time 386 s on this machine).

## Definition → corpus mapping

| Exhibit object | Corpus object |
|---|---|
| Configuration: phases θ(x,μ) ∈ [0,2π), x ∈ (ℤ/6)⁴, μ ∈ Fin 4; U = e^{iθ} | `Config Λ G = (Λ × Fin 4) → G` with Λ = `Fin 4 → ZMod 6`, G = `Circle` (`LatticeGauge.lean` §§1, 8) |
| Plaquette phase θ_p = θ(x,μ) + θ(x+μ̂,ν) − θ(x+ν̂,μ) − θ(x,ν) | Ordered plaquette `U_p(x,μ,ν) = U(x,μ)·U(σ_μ x,ν)·(U(σ_ν x,μ))⁻¹·(U(x,ν))⁻¹` (`LatticeGauge.lean`, design-choice docstring) |
| Action density per plaquette: 1 − cos θ_p | `1 − (1/d) Re tr ρ(U_p)` at d = 1, fundamental rep of `Circle` (same docstring) |
| Weight of a configuration: exp(−β Σ_p (1 − cos θ_p)) | `gibbsMeasure`: `Z⁻¹ • configMeasure.withDensity (exp (−S_β))`, S_β = `wilsonAction` (`LatticeCorrelations.lean`, `gibbsDensity`/`gibbsMeasure`, ll. 175–190); `configMeasure` = product of normalized Haar over links |
| β = 0 ensemble | The β = 0 package of Stage 0: product Haar, exact statements proved for general (G, ρ) |
| Wilson loops W(R×R) | Closed-path Wilson loops (`LatticeGauge.lean` §4) |

## Sampler and validation

- **β = 0 is exact sampling, not MCMC.** Each link phase is drawn
  independently uniform — product Haar itself. The only error is
  finite-sample noise in the estimates.
- **β > 0: heatbath.** The conditional of one link given its neighbours
  is a von Mises distribution with concentration β·|z|, z the complex
  staple sum over the 6 plaquettes containing the link. The staple
  identity (Σ cos φ_p over those plaquettes = Re[e^{iθ}·z]) was
  verified numerically against the corpus plaquette convention to
  3×10⁻¹⁵ over 120 random probes before any production run. Links are
  updated in parity classes that share no plaquette, so each sweep is
  an exact Gibbs sweep.
- **Protocol (per β > 0):** 300 thermalization sweeps discarded, then
  3,000 configurations recorded (one per sweep), β continued upward
  from the previous β's last configuration. Errors are batch-means
  SEMs (batch = 100 sweeps). Per β the curve uses 3,000 equilibrated
  configurations; β = 0 Wilson loops use 600 exact configurations.
- **Exact anchors, both confirmed:** ⟨Re U_p⟩ at β = 0 is
  +0.00013 ± 0.00012 (theory: exactly 0); ⟨Re W(R×R)⟩ at β = 0 is
  +0.0001 / −0.0002 / +0.0002 (± 0.0003) for R = 1/2/3 (theory: exactly
  0 — the fundamental representation has no Haar-fixed vectors, which
  is the corpus's Stage 0 β = 0 theorem read numerically).
- **One honest blemish, disclosed:** β = 1.0 sits inside the
  finite-volume crossover region and fluctuates slowly there. The
  production (continuation) run gives ⟨Re U_p⟩ = 0.6052 ± 0.0046; an
  independent cold-start run under the identical protocol gives
  0.5946 ± 0.0034. Read the β = 1.0 point's true uncertainty as ≈ 0.01,
  larger than its printed batch SEM. All other curve points have SEMs
  ≤ 0.0002 and no start-dependence was observed in spot checks.

## The amendment (Jeff, the commissioner, mid-build)

Jeff's spec sentence, verbatim:

> "the exact different from the thermal bath and the electron density
> cloud — heat + electron − vacuum portrait."

Two panels were added on the same sampler, same lattice, same seeds
discipline:

**Panel 5 — `portrait_difference.png`, the difference portrait.**
Ensemble-averaged plaquette action density on the slice at β = 1.0
(the thermal configuration) minus the ensemble average at β = 3.0,
labelled on the figure and here as what it is: *the vacuum reference —
the ordered end of the sweep, not T = 0 itself.* Diverging scale
centred exactly at 0; difference range on the slice
[+0.2833, +0.3231] about the volume-mean difference 0.3075. The
near-flatness of this panel is itself the finding, and it is forced by
translation invariance: with nothing inserted, the thermal excitation
content of this toy is *delocalised* — a uniform sheet of extra action
density, not a lump. A single portrait (Panels 1–2) is dominated by
vacuum fluctuation; the subtraction is what exposes the excitation
content at all. This is the standard logic of **normal ordering and
the Casimir effect** (T4 precedent): the vacuum's contribution is not
observable; *differences* from it are.

**Panel 6 — `portrait_flux_tube.png`, the content portrait.**
Jeff's "electron cloud" enters the only way it honestly can in a pure
gauge theory: as **static external charges**, represented by a
rectangular Wilson loop (R = 3 × T = 3 in the slice plane, β = 2.0).
Method (stated on the figure): reweighting,
⟨D⟩_loop − ⟨D⟩ = ⟨D·Re W⟩/⟨Re W⟩ − ⟨D⟩ over the 3,000-config β = 2.0
ensemble. The signal survived its noise audit, so the reweighted panel
is what shipped: ⟨Re W(3×3)⟩ = +0.5173 ± 0.0110; the conditioned
density difference peaks at |Δ| = 0.0176 against a median batch SEM of
0.0033 (≈ 5× the noise floor), range [−0.0176, +0.0055]. Reading: in an
*abelian* theory the loop is exactly the product of its interior
plaquettes (Stokes), so conditioning on a large loop value selects
aligned interior plaquettes — the dip inside the gold outline is that
Stokes correlation made visible, not a nonabelian flux tube's energy
profile. The fallback (single-config difference) was not needed and
was not shipped.

## Fences (read before citing any picture)

- **U(1) only — not SU(3), not QCD, not the Standard Model.**
- **L = 6 toy torus at fixed lattice spacing. No continuum limit is
  taken or implied.**
- **Every portrait is a 2D slice (x² = x³ = 0) of a 4D object, or an
  ensemble average of such slices; the projection is stated on each
  figure.**
- **Samples are not the theory.** A configuration is one draw from the
  measure; the theory is the measure itself.
- **No mass-gap claim of any kind** — lattice or continuum. Nothing
  here touches Stage 3c's uniformity question.
- **This exhibit is PURE GAUGE.** The "electron density cloud" of the
  amendment enters only as static external charges through the Wilson
  loop of Panel 6. There are no dynamical fermions anywhere in this
  build; a dynamical electron-cloud exhibit is a different build.
- **β = 3.0 is a vacuum *reference*, not T = 0.**
