# Shipment note — the vacuum portrait exhibit (2026-10-18)

**What this is, in one line:** six numerical portraits of the U(1)
lattice vacuum, drawn by sampling the Gibbs measure this corpus
itself defines and proves theorems about — an exhibit of *samples*,
not a theorem and not evidence for any claim.

## What was sampled (definition → corpus, in brief)

The finite Gibbs measure of Pitch 4, Stage 0/1a
(`gibbsMeasure` in `LatticeCorrelations.lean`: the product-Haar
configuration measure reweighted by exp(−β·`wilsonAction`), with
plaquettes and Wilson loops as in `LatticeGauge.lean`), instantiated
for the circle group U(1) with its fundamental representation on a
6⁴ periodic torus (L = 6). Sampler: exact independent draws at
β = 0 (product Haar itself); heatbath with exact per-link von Mises
conditionals at β > 0 (300 thermalization sweeps, 3,000 recorded
configurations per β; fixed seed 20261018). The full
definition-to-corpus mapping, protocol, and tier table are in
[EXHIBIT.md](EXHIBIT.md), shipped alongside and unchanged.

## Theorem anchors (samples checked against proved values)

- **β = 0:** ⟨Re U_p⟩ sampled at **+0.00013 ± 0.00012** against the
  Stage 0 theorem value **exactly 0** (product Haar, general
  (G, ρ)); Wilson loops R = 1, 2, 3 likewise within ±0.0003 of 0.
- **β = 0.25:** ⟨Re U_p⟩ sampled at **0.12404 ± 0.00012** against
  the Stage 1c-0 proved first-order value **0.125**
  (⟨W_p⟩_β = β/2 + O(β²) at the circle).

## Fences (verbatim in substance from EXHIBIT.md — read before citing any picture)

- **U(1) only — not SU(3), not QCD, not the Standard Model.**
- **L = 6 toy torus at fixed lattice spacing. No continuum limit is
  taken or implied.**
- **Every portrait is a 2D slice (x² = x³ = 0) of a 4D object, or an
  ensemble average of such slices; the projection is stated on each
  figure.**
- **Samples are not the theory.** A configuration is one draw from the
  measure; the theory is the measure itself.
- **No mass-gap claim of any kind** — lattice or continuum. Nothing
  here touches Stage 3c's uniformity question.
- **This exhibit is PURE GAUGE.** The "electron density cloud" enters
  only as static external charges through the Wilson loop of the
  flux-tube panel. There are no dynamical fermions anywhere in this
  build; a dynamical electron-cloud exhibit is a different build.
- **β = 3.0 is a vacuum *reference*, not T = 0.**

## Disclosed blemish

β = 1.0 sits inside the finite-volume crossover region and
fluctuates slowly there: the production (continuation) run gives
⟨Re U_p⟩ = 0.6052 ± 0.0046, an independent cold-start run under the
identical protocol gives 0.5946 ± 0.0034. Read the β = 1.0 point's
true uncertainty as ≈ 0.01, larger than its printed batch SEM. All
other curve points have SEMs ≤ 0.0002.
