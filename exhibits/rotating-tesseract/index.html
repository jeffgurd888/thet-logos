Exhibit — a picture, not a proof

Labeled plainly: this is an exhibit — a picture of the mathematics, not a proof. The exact part is the arithmetic: a 4-dimensional cube has 16 corners, 32 edges, 24 faces, and 8 cube-shaped cells, and no rotation changes the count.

It runs right here in your browser. No account, no install, no tracking.

Free forever, for everyone — one common bond.

A 4D rotation doesn't turn around an axis; it turns in a plane, and four dimensions have six planes. Turn two disjoint planes at once — a double rotation — and the inner cell swells, passes through the outer cell, and takes its place. That inside-out pass is the signature no 3D object can perform.
