Planes inside ordinary 3D space

Planes through the fourth direction, w

Each slider is one rotation plane's speed. Center is stopped; drag left of center and that plane runs backwards. A rigid model in your hand can only turn in the first three — the w-planes are the ones that exist only here.

A 4D rotation doesn't turn around an axis; it turns in a plane, and four dimensions have six planes. Turn two disjoint planes at once — a double rotation — and the inner cell swells, passes through the outer cell, and takes its place. That inside-out pass is the signature no 3D object can perform.
