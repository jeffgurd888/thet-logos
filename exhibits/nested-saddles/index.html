2 · The climb back, first toll — phase

Two wavefunctions, one shadow. On the right, every lobe's sign has been flipped across its node — the twin is ψ with its phase pattern changed, so |ψ|² is identical, point for point. Their shadows cannot tell them apart. Nothing in the shadow can.

The shadow dropped the phase. Interference is how you buy it back. Add one known reference wave and let it interfere: the fringe pattern shifts exactly where the signs differ, and the twins stop looking alike. The information was never in the shadow — it has to be supplied.
