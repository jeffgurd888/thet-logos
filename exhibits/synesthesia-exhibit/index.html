Panel 4 — Rainbow dispersion (water drop, one internal reflection)

T4 computed curve · Descartes minimum deviation
Physical color is the data on this panel: the curve is drawn in the visible spectrum’s own colors, and the exhibit color law is not applied on top of it — this panel says so. Transformations, all named: refractive index n(λ) from the named Sellmeier coefficient set for liquid water at 20 °C (IAPWS R9-97 lineage; Daimon & Masumoto 2012 tabulation; coefficients printed in the generator script); scattering angle from Descartes’ minimum-deviation formula for one internal reflection, θ = π − D(i*), cos i* = √((n² − 1)/3), measured from the antisolar point; segment colors are an approximate wavelength-to-RGB rendering for display (a named display choice). Computed curve — no observation. Computed 2026-11-01.
