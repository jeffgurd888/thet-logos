Exhibit — a picture, not a proof

Labeled plainly: this is an exhibit — a picture of the mathematics, not a proof. The theorem it pictures is machine-checked in Lean 4 (PhaseWinding.lean): winding numbers add in ℤ, a single-valued (gauge) shift cannot move the winding number, and a phase slip moves it by exactly the slip. What you see here is that algebra, drawn live.

The exact part is the ledger at the top: steps + 12 × slips = 12 × w + position in the turn, true at every moment, however you play. Ordinary stepping never moves w except by completing a turn — then by exactly 1 — and no relabeling of the sites can change it. Only a phase slip moves w suddenly, and then by exactly the slip, never more, never less.

The fences, stated plainly: this is a finite ring of 12 sites and an integer counter — no manifold, no order-parameter field, no vortex core. It is not an Abrikosov vortex, and it does not model any physical vortex or any physical device; it illustrates the winding atom such systems carry, in the exact form the corpus proves it.
