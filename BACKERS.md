# Bench Backers — inscribed in the permanent record

Jeff's promise, made publicly on 2026-10-25 under the Special
Report post: donations to this work are not investments and carry
no promised return — nobody honest can promise that, and this
program publishes its zeros. What a backer receives is simpler
and keepable: **their name inscribed here, in the permanent
record, alongside the work they backed** — stored for everyone,
free, forever, per the Foundation.

The bench currently runs on the founder's plasma donations
(twice a week) and donor support. Donations:
[Cash App $Gurd888](https://cash.app/$Gurd888).

## The standing promise (Jeff, 2026-10-25)

**Every donation will be published with the proof stream.**
Each new landing's dossier carries a "Backers since last landing"
section, and this file is the ledger of record. Three rails keep
the promise keepable, and they are part of the promise:

1. **As the giver wishes.** A name appears exactly as its owner
   wants — full name, initials, a dedication, or Anonymous. An
   amount is published only with the giver's okay; otherwise the
   entry reads "a gift of bench time."
2. **On Jeff's confirmation.** Donations arrive where only Jeff
   can see them (Cash App); an entry is inscribed when Jeff
   confirms it. A donation surfaced late is inscribed
   retroactively, dated by its gift, and the correction is part
   of the record — the ledger corrects itself in the open.
3. **Exactness.** The ledger states only what happened: no
   donation is published that did not arrive, and none known is
   omitted. The Concrete applies to money.
