# REVIEW.md — the statement-fidelity review pack

**For an outside expert with one hour.** This corpus's proofs are
machine-checked by the Lean kernel; the kernel cannot judge whether
a proved statement *says what we say it says*. That judgement —
statement fidelity — is the one layer of this work that has never
had an outside referee. This pack exists to make it easy to attack.

**How to use it.** Each row below is a load-bearing public claim,
the exact Lean statement it rests on (theorem name + file), its
tier, and the most concrete way we can imagine the row failing
fidelity — where we would press if we were reviewing us. Pick any
row, open the file, read the statement (not our prose), and press.
Running `lean/scripts/verify-external.sh` first confirms you are
looking at the corpus we think we published.

**Filing a finding.** Open an issue with the *External review*
template (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/external-review.md`): what you
checked, your method, your verdict, your evidence. Every received
review is logged verbatim in `EXTERNAL-REVIEWS.md`, answered in the
open, and — if you confirm a fidelity failure — recorded in the
epistemic ledger as a kill at the same volume as a landing. A kill
is a result in this house, not a rejection.

**Tiers.** T1 = machine-checked proof of the statement as written.
KILL (T1) = a machine-checked *refutation* we publish as a claim.
NO CLAIM rows are claims of *absence* — they fail in the opposite
direction, and the table says what would break them.

**Where we would start.** Rows 2, 10, and 13 carry the most prose
weight per line of statement. Rows 23–25 are the cheapest to attack
and the most damaging if they fall: they need one exhibited theorem,
not an afternoon of reading.

**What a review is worth here.** Every confirmed finding — in
either direction — is logged verbatim in `EXTERNAL-REVIEWS.md`,
answered in the open, and entered in the epistemic ledger at the
same rank as our own landings and kills. That is not a courtesy;
it is the mechanism this corpus uses to stay honest.
