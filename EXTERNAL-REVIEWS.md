# EXTERNAL-REVIEWS.md — the public ledger of external reviews

This file is the record of every review of this corpus received
from outside the program: verification runs, statement-fidelity
findings, priority finds, failures to reproduce. It exists so that
the sentence "this work has been peer reviewed" can one day be
said with receipts — or never said at all.

**Rules of this ledger (printed on its face, binding on us):**

1. Reviews are logged **verbatim**, with date and source, under
   the reviewer's stated permission (see the *External review*
   issue template). A review is never edited after logging.
2. Our response is **appended** beneath the review it answers —
   never written over it, never merged into it.
3. A confirmed statement-fidelity failure is recorded in the
   epistemic ledger as a **kill, at the same volume as a
   landing**, and the affected public claims are corrected at
   full rank. A kill is a result in this house, not a rejection.
4. Curation, citation, and social-media engagement are **not**
   reviews and are not logged as reviews. Being selected,
   linked, or quoted is not being refereed.

mistaken for reviews of this work:

  other direction.** *We* independently rebuilt Lianghao Cao's
  hierarchical-laminates repository from a clean checkout under
  a pre-registered protocol: pins re-verified at fire time, all
  222 modules rebuilt clean, the axiom audit reproduced with
  exactly {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound} across 3,493
  declarations, headline declarations re-checked by name. It
  passed on build and axiom claims; the statement-fidelity
  column did not move — a rebuild cannot judge fidelity. This
  is the treatment `verify-external.sh` is built to make easy
  for anyone aiming it at *this* corpus. It is not a review of
  our work; nobody has done to our corpus what we did to his.

  review.** The AB-7 shipment (finite occupation entropy) was
