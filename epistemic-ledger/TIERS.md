# The Five-Tier Epistemic Ledger

Source: Gurd, *The Ontological Thet–LOGOS Framework* (Revised Draft, Nexus
Research, Sept 2026), §5. Attribution: **Jeffrey Michael Gurd, Nexus
Research.**

All claims in this repository are classified under these five tiers to
prevent category errors. **Use exactly these tiers; do not invent new
ones.** Evidence qualifiers in parentheses (e.g. "Lean proof", "numerical")
describe *how* a tier was earned — they are not separate tiers.

## Axiom 5.1 — Strict Epistemic Rule (Framework §5)

```
Theorem  ≠  Simulation  ≠  Experiment  ≠  Device
```

Machine-checked or numerical verification of algebraic identities
establishes **consistency of the representation**; it does not constitute
physical evidence. Physical predictions must be tested against independent
empirical data.

## The tiers

| Tier | Category | Included elements |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Assumed Primitives | Void ∅, pair (θ,θ†), N₀³ = N₀ |
| **T2** | Defined Operations | TRO ⟨a,b,c⟩, K = −log ∆, D_F, π |
| **T3** | Proved / Checked | Order-zero identity (numerical); standard NCG facts |
| **T4** | Emergent / Standard | Vanishing cross-terms under order-one; Yukawa form |
| **T5** | Open / Withdrawn | dim ker L = 4 (withdrawn); coupling unification; mass predictions; three-generation extension; formal Möbius topology; complete Lean archive |

## Reading guide

- **T1** items are assumed, never proved. In Lean they appear as `axiom`.
- **T2** items are definitions. In Lean they are `def`/`structure`.
- **T3** items are proved or checked. Evidence is noted per entry:
  `(Lean proof)`, `(numerical)`, or `(Lean statement; proof pending)`.
  A `sorry` is a T3 *statement*, never a proof.
- **T4** items are standard results of the NCG literature reproduced here;
  no novelty is claimed for them.
- **T5** items are open problems or **withdrawn** claims. Withdrawn items
  are recorded explicitly so they are not accidentally re-asserted.
