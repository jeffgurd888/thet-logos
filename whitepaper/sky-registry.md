## §0 — the ceiling (verbatim from the scoping of record)

The strongest outcome this instrument can ever produce is a registry
whose verdicts — **including a full row of NO-CORRELATION** — are
claimable at T1/T4 discipline: every comparison pre-registered, every
owned value a landed theorem or a registered cross-check, every sky
value a citable public datum, every tolerance frozen before the data

named pairs and reports what it finds, at the same volume either way.

moves no dial by construction, and — the fence this charter exists
to state — **a correlation that survives pre-registration is a
registered correlation, NOT evidence for the seeing.** Promotion to
evidence is Dial 4's separate, higher gate (§5); this registry is
not that door and does not open onto it.

## §4 — the admission protocol and comparison shape (standing)

An admissible comparison has exactly this shape, **all elements
frozen in a mini-scoping written and dated before the sky datum is
consulted in the comparing direction**:

1. **A named pair**: one owned quantity (module + theorem, or BR1
   entry ID) and one sky quantity (catalog + column/statistic, by
   formula). The pair list is closed under nothing — no "and
   similar quantities," no families, no substitutions.
2. **Type-honesty**: dimensionless against dimensionless, with the
   dimensionless combination written out (not "a ratio to be
   determined").
3. **A fixed tolerance**, stated as a number with its justification
   (e.g. the sky datum's published uncertainty). A tolerance chosen
   after seeing the difference is not a tolerance.
4. **A multiplicity budget**: the total number of pairs in the
   registry at the time of the run. A verdict of "agreement" is
   reported *with* its budget ("1 agreement in 1 registered pair"),
   never bare.
5. **Exactness at the verdict line** where the owned side is
   rational (BRIDGE-1 discipline); sky-side floats admitted only
   inside published uncertainties the tolerance must dominate.

**Admission gate (standing, replaces a queue).** A proposed pair
enters only as its own mini-scoping (effort class S) re-running
§2's three screens and this section's five elements for that pair
alone. If it passes, the pair is appended here and exactly one
comparison run is priced at that time. If it fails any screen, the
mini-scoping is filed as a **rejected admission** (§6) — rejections
are registered too, so the pair list's emptiness stays auditable
rather than merely asserted. The order of knowledge is part of the
registration: a pair proposed by someone who already knows both
values is admissible only if that knowledge is disclosed in the
entry, and the tolerance is then justified from the sky datum's
uncertainty alone.

Data is fetched **only** inside an admitted pair's comparison run,
from the citable sources of §2, and the fetched snapshot's
provenance (catalog version, query, date) becomes part of that
entry. Downloading catalog snapshots "so the data is ready" is
refused: with no registered pair, holding the data is the dredging
temptation made concrete.

Unregistered sky-comparison numbers do not ship in dossiers or
posts, at any tier (BRIDGE-2's standing rule, applied by analogy).

## §5 — the Dial-4 boundary (stated exactly)

**A correlation registry entry compares two datasets that are both
already fixed. Nothing in it was predicted before an outcome, no
future measurement is at stake, and no external party grades
anything. It therefore cannot move Dial 4 — not at any sample
size, not at any significance. A row of agreements in this registry
is a row of registered correlations, full stop.**

A Dial-4-grade entry reachable from this instrument's material
would have to look like this, and only this: the owned side
supplies a number frozen in this registry with its theorem cited;
a *separate* entry in the **prediction registry** (BRIDGE-3's file,
five fields, K-BR3 in force) states, in advance and with a date,
that a named future external measurement will find a named
quantity within a named range derived from the frozen owned
number; and an external party's published measurement then lands
inside (Dial 4 moves) or outside (the entry retires by its own
kill reading). This registry's role in that chain is custodial
only. **No current candidate meets this bar** (§2: the quantities
definite enough to freeze predict nothing about any named future
measurement; the quantities that resemble sky data are
chosen-input). Stated at full rank so no future row of this
registry is ever quoted as "almost Dial 4."
