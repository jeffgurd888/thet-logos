## The claim

A particle's mass *is* the tick rate of repeated thet action.
$m = \hbar\omega/c^2$ read backwards: $\omega = mc^2/\hbar$ is the harmonic
at which the thet process repeats to sustain the structure we call a particle.

Physics already whispers this: Dirac's zitterbewegung — the electron trembles
at $2mc^2/\hbar \approx 2.5 \times 10^{20}$ Hz. The de Broglie clock. Mass was
always a frequency; the language just says so out loud.

## The unfolding

**0** — the void. Nothing corresponds.

**.** — 0 . 0. Thet distinguishes itself from the void. The seed.

**{ | }** — the first correspondence. An action happens at `|`; `{ }` are left
behind as its traces. The relata do not precede; they follow.

**e(ω)** — the action repeats harmonically at frequency ω. Most repetitions
dissipate into the void — incoherent action leaves no standing trace.

**Resonance** — at certain frequencies the repetitions reinforce instead of
canceling. The repeat operator locks: $R\psi = \lambda\psi$. A standing
pattern in thet-action. *This is the particle.*

**m** — the locked harmonic has a tick rate ω; what physics calls mass is
$m = \hbar\omega/c^2$. Inertia is then the resistance of a locked harmonic to
being driven off resonance — like pushing a swing at the wrong frequency.

## The electron, unfolded

1. 0 → . → { | }: thet's bootstrap, as in the language spec.
2. Harmonic repetition at $\omega_e = m_e c^2/\hbar \approx 7.8 \times 10^{20}\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$.
3. Resonance locks. Stable structure: charge −e, spin 1/2, mass $m_e$.
4. (Hypothesis) Charge is the *orientation* of the correspondence — which way
   the `|` "points" between `{` and `}`. Spin is the harmonic's handedness
   under repetition. Both are properties *of the repeating*, not of a thing.
