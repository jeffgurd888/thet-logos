## (a) The general equivalence machinery — proved vs fenced

Setting: `T : E →ₗ.[𝕜] E`, `E` a Hilbert space over an `RCLike` field,
`T` densely defined and symmetric in Campaign 2's proved sense
`T ≤ T†` (`LinearPMap.adjoint`, notation `†`).

**PROVED (all in `BosonicESA.lean`, pure order/graph arguments):**

1. `closed_closure_eq_self` — a closed partial map equals its own
   closure. (Small API gap filled: Mathlib has the graph
   characterization but not this statement.)
2. `symmetric_isClosable` — densely defined + symmetric ⇒ closable:
   the adjoint is a closed extension (`adjoint_isClosed`), and a map
   with a closed extension is closable.
3. `symmetric_closure_le_adjoint` — `T.closure ≤ T†`: the closure is
   the smallest closed extension and `T†` is one
   (`IsClosable.closure_mono` + item 1).
4. `closure_eq_adjoint_iff_adjoint_le_closure` — **(i) ⟺ (ii)**:
   `T.closure = T†` ⟺ `T† ≤ T.closure`. Pure logic from item 3.
5. `submodule_adjoint_topologicalClosure_eq` — a graph and its
   topological closure have the same `Submodule.adjoint`: the pairing
   condition of `Submodule.mem_adjoint_iff` is a closed condition on
   `E × F` (continuity of the inner product), so it propagates from
   the graph to its closure.
6. `adjoint_closure_eq_adjoint` — **`(T.closure)† = T†`** for densely
   defined `T`, via items 5 and `adjoint_graph_eq_graph_adjoint`.
   This is the closure-side half of von Neumann's theorem, and it is
   pure graph theory. (Also `dense_domain_closure`: the closure's
   domain stays dense, from `le_closure`.)
7. `closure_eq_adjoint_iff_isSelfAdjoint_closure` — `T.closure = T†`
   ⟺ `IsSelfAdjoint T.closure`, via item 6. (The gauntlet's
   "equivalently `IsSelfAdjoint hamPMap.closure`" form — now a theorem,
   not a remark.)
8. `adjoint_le_adjoint_adjoint_of_closure_eq_adjoint` — **(i) ⟹
   (iii)**: if `T.closure = T†` then `T† ≤ T††` — indeed `T†† = T†`,
   by item 6 applied through the equality.

**FENCED, by name (one item):**

9. `closure_eq_adjoint_iff_adjoint_le_adjoint_adjoint_of_doubleAdjoint`
   — the implication **(iii) ⟹ (i)** and hence the full three-way
   equivalence hold *conditionally* on the hypothesis
   `hVN : T†† = T.closure`. That identity is the other half of von
   Neumann's theorem (the double-adjoint theorem), and it is **absent
   from the pinned Mathlib**: there is no `LinearPMap.adjoint_adjoint`
   (grep-verified over the whole checkout, 2026-10-17; only the
   `ContinuousLinearMap`/`LinearMap` finite analogues exist). The
   conditional is stated and proved, so the fence has a precise
   address: any future Mathlib (or corpus) proof of `T†† = T.closure`
   for densely defined closable `T` discharges it by application.
   ESA-β does **not** route through this identity; R2 argues on
   domains directly (§(c)).

## (b) The instantiated target

```lean
def BosonicESA (d : ℕ) (lam : Fin d → ℝ)
    (W : Fin d → Fin d → Fin d → Fin d → ℂ) (g : ℝ) : Prop :=
  (hamPMap d lam W g).closure = (hamPMap d lam W g)†
```

with `hamPMap` from `BosonicInteraction.lean:1092`. Proved
restatements, all instantiations of §(a) fed by Campaign 2's
`hamPMap_le_adjoint` (:1108) and `dom_dense` (:1087) — the new
theorem content beyond C2 is exactly the closure side:

- `hamPMap_isClosable` — `hamPMap` is closable.
- `hamPMap_closure_le_adjoint` — `(hamPMap).closure ≤ (hamPMap)†`:
  one inclusion of `BosonicESA` is already a theorem.
- `hamPMap_closure_isClosed` — the closure is a closed operator.
- `bosonicESA_iff_adjoint_le_closure` — **`BosonicESA` ⟺
  `(hamPMap)† ≤ (hamPMap).closure`**: the entire remaining content of
  Pitch 3's open clause is this one inclusion.
- `bosonicESA_iff_isSelfAdjoint_closure` — `BosonicESA` ⟺
  `IsSelfAdjoint (hamPMap).closure`.
- `bosonicESA_adjoint_le_adjoint_adjoint` — `BosonicESA` would imply
  `(hamPMap)† ≤ (hamPMap)††` (direction (iii), proved); the converse
  is the fenced item 9.

## (c) The ESA-β map — what a proof must supply, step by step

By `bosonicESA_iff_adjoint_le_closure`, a proof of `BosonicESA` must
supply exactly `(hamPMap)† ≤ (hamPMap).closure`, i.e.:

- **Domain:** `Dom (hamPMap)† ⊆ Dom (hamPMap).closure`;
- **Action:** agreement of the closure with the adjoint on that
  domain (automatic once the graph inclusion below is shown:
  `LinearPMap.le_of_le_graph`).

Equivalently, the core statement: **every `y ∈ Dom (hamPMap)†` is a
graph-norm limit of finite-particle vectors** — there are
`yₙ ∈ Dom d` with `yₙ → y` and `hamIntBos yₙ → (hamPMap)† y`.
(`Dom d` a core for the adjoint.) Route **R2** (banded truncation,
gauntlet-recommended; R1 held in reserve):

**β1 — Adjoint-domain sector decomposition (price: M; the risk
concentration).** Show that `y ∈ Dom (hamPMap)†` decomposes
sectorwise: each sector component `yₙ ∈ domSector d n` (finite
dimensional) satisfies: the sector component of `(hamPMap)† y` is
`hamIntBosSector d lam W g n yₙ`.
*Inputs present:* the Hamiltonian preserves every sector
(`hamIntBos_mem_domSector`, `BosonicInteraction.lean:994`), and the
underlying reason is sharper than the gauntlet's general band: the
quartic monomial is the balanced `a†a†aa` form and **preserves total
particle number exactly** — `monoCoef_ne_zero_tot` (:323):
`monoCoef d i j k l γ ≠ 0 → tot (monoPhi d i j k l γ) = tot γ`. The
band is **Δn = 0**, not {−4, −2, 0, 2, 4} (that wider band describes
an arbitrary quartic Wick monomial; the corpus's symmetrized quartic
never leaves its sector). Sectorwise self-adjointness is
`hamIntBosSector_spectral` (C2), so on each finite-dimensional sector
the adjoint is the sector operator itself. The pairing argument is:
for `x ∈ Dom d` in sector `n`, `⟪(hamPMap)† y, x⟫ = ⟪y, hamPMap x⟫
= ⟪yₙ, Hₙ x⟫ = ⟪Hₙ yₙ, x⟫` — sector orthogonality + the
finite-dimensional adjoint.
*API census:* the adjoint-domain membership criterion exists
(`LinearPMap.mem_adjoint_domain_iff`, continuity form) and the graph
characterization exists (`adjoint_graph_eq_graph_adjoint`), but
**no Mathlib API decomposes an unbounded operator or its adjoint over
a direct sum of subspaces** — this step unwinds the criterion by hand
against the corpus's own sector decomposition of `BosonicFock d`
(`lp` over `Config d`; `domSector`, `FockLimit`/`BosonicAssembly`
material). Hand-built, corpus-local. That unwinding is the step.

**β2 — The ℓ² tail estimate (price: S–M).** Given β1, with
`z = (hamPMap)† y`: `Σₙ ‖yₙ‖² < ∞` (y is a Fock vector) and
`Σₙ ‖Hₙ yₙ‖² = Σₙ ‖zₙ‖² < ∞` (z is a Fock vector). The finite
truncations `y^{(≤N)} ∈ Dom d` then converge to `y` in graph norm:
both `‖y − y^{(≤N)}‖²` and `‖z − hamPMap y^{(≤N)}‖²` are tails of
convergent ℓ² series. Note the sharpening the Δn = 0 band buys: this
is **tail-vanishing of two convergent series, not a comparison
estimate** — the `(n+2)²` number-relative bound (B1) is *not* needed
here. That bound is R1's input (Nelson/Faris–Lavine comparison
`(number+1)²`), and remains load-bearing for B3(ii) (already proved);
if β1's unwinding fails, R1 is the fallback at **L** and would spend
it. *API census:* `lp`/`Memℓp` API is present in Mathlib; expect
friction expressing truncations as finite-support approximations in
the corpus's presentation, but no missing concepts.

**β3 — Assembly (price: S).** β1+β2 put every graph pair
`(y, (hamPMap)† y)` in `graph(hamPMap).topologicalClosure`, which is
`graph((hamPMap).closure)` by `IsClosable.graph_closure_eq_closure_graph`
(API present); `LinearPMap.le_of_le_graph` concludes
`(hamPMap)† ≤ (hamPMap).closure`, and
`bosonicESA_iff_adjoint_le_closure` closes `BosonicESA`. All API
present; pure assembly.

**β total: M–L stands** (the gauntlet's price), with the risk now
located precisely in β1's hand-unwinding rather than in a tail
comparison estimate.
