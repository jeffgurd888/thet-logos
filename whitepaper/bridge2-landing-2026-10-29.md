(the cross-check registry, entries BR1-001…BR1-004), BRIDGE-2
makes the registry a mandatory item on every future probe's
landing checklist. The amendment is written into
`whitepaper/cross-check-registry.md` ("BRIDGE-2 adoption —
the landing-checklist amendment (2026-10-29)"), on its face:

- every probe landing names its registry entry ID in its
  dossier, or registers a new entry before its dossier ships;
  unregistered probe numbers do not ship in dossiers or posts;

  per K-BR1 and says so in its dossier;
- the registry step sits inside the landing ceremony (parent
  verification → registry entry → dossier → README → commit →
  push).

registered as part of its landing earlier today); the rule
now binds every successor probe.
