## Named differences from the predicted shape (full rank)

The scoping predicted `A₅·κ₅^dep + A₄·κ₄^dep + Σ c_ch·Excess_ch`
with A₅, A₄ polynomials in M and B. The true identity differs
in two named ways — recorded on the module's face, not
smoothed:

1. **The κ₅ dressing factor is exactly the order-5 factor**
   `4 + (M−4)·B` — the direct channel-excess contributions
   of the doubled fibers cancel identically against the
   κ₅^dep rewrite, so no standalone `kappa5chanExcess` term
   survives. (A confirmation, not a discrepancy — but it was
   not predicted, so it is recorded.)
2. **A₄ is not a polynomial in M, B alone**: it carries
   `½·(M−4)·μ₂/d²`, from the doubled-penta fiber (at a
   5-support the doubled private column strips off
   `realMoment 2`, and its erasure excess sums to
   `(M−4)·Φ₄`).
3. **One extra term**: `−d⁻⁶·e₁²·S4theta`, where S4theta sums
   the quad excess over the rectangle 4-subsets of Θ
   supports. A Θ support's rectangle 4-subsets ARE primitive
   quads, so this sum does not reduce to a multiple of
   κ₄^dep by any corpus counting; the companion fact is
   proved in-file (a C₆ support has NO primitive 4-subset,
   so the C₆ side of the split contributes nothing here).

Every one of the eleven fiber sums of `betaCoeff6_total`
found a dictionary entry; no term was absorbed silently,
and K-B6-0 never re-engaged (no missed channel).
