## What is proved (all T1, machine-checked)

**§1–§2. Setup and the elementary Taylor bound.** The Wilson action factors as
`wilsonAction σ ρ β U = β * actionDensity σ ρ U` (by `rfl` against the Stage-0
definition), with `actionDensity` bounded by the explicit
`actionBound = 2 * (16 * Fintype.card Λ)`. The exponential estimate is the calculus
bound |e^{−x} − (1 − x)| ≤ (x²/2)·e^{|x|}, applied to numerator and denominator
separately — no asymptotic hand-waving anywhere in the module.

**§3. First-order expansion with explicit constant (clause iv + the derivative half
of (i) of the commission).** For any bounded measurable observable F with ‖F‖ ≤ B and
|β| ≤ 1:

  ‖⟨F⟩_β − (⟨F⟩₀ − β·Cov₀(F, A))‖ ≤ firstOrderConst(B) · β²,

where A is the cast action density and the constant is the closed expression

  firstOrderConst(B) = e^{A_b} · ( B·(A_b²/2 · e^{A_b}) + 2·B·A_b²
                                 + (B + 2·B·A_b)·(A_b²/2 · e^{A_b}) ),
  A_b := actionBound = 2·(16·|Λ|).

Corollaries: `hasDerivAt_gibbsExpect_zero` — the derivative of a Gibbs expectation at
β = 0 is `−Cov₀(F, A)` (the derivative-of-expectation identity, proved from the Gibbs
density at fixed finite volume), and specifically for a plaquette,
`deriv_plaquetteLoop_eq`: d/dβ ⟨W_p⟩_β |₀ = −Σ_{q sharing} Cov₀(W_p, D_q).

**§4. Analyticity (clause i).** `analyticAt_gibbsExpect`: β ↦ ⟨F⟩_β is real-analytic at
0, via a complex power series for numerator and denominator with the explicit radius
(2·max(actionBound, 1))⁻¹, the denominator nonzero at 0 (value 1), and real restriction.
Analyticity is at the point 0 only, at fixed volume; no radius uniform in volume is
claimed or implied.

**§5. The link-sharing reduction (the general half of clause ii).** For any observable
depending only on a link set s, the Stage-1a β=0 factorization
(`gibbsExpect_zero_mul_of_disjoint`) kills every covariance term whose plaquette is
disjoint from s. Hence, with `sharingPlaquettes σ s` the plaquettes meeting s:

  Cov₀(F, A) = Σ_{q ∈ sharingPlaquettes} Cov₀(F, D_q),

an exact finite sum of explicit covariance integrals — in particular
`covZero_plaquetteLoop_eq_sum` for F = W_p. Each surviving term is bounded by
‖Cov₀(W_p, D_q)‖ ≤ 4·d.

**§6. Perimeter law at first order (clause iii, torus).** On the torus
Λ = Fin 4 → ZMod N, a fixed link lies in at most 12 plaquettes (four slots × three
choices of the second direction), so |(sharingPlaquettes)| ≤ 12·|s| for any support s, and

  ‖Cov₀(W(C), A)‖ ≤ 48·d·|∂C|

where |∂C| is the number of links of the loop C: the first-order response of a Wilson
loop sees its **perimeter** (its link count) linearly, with per-link constant 48·d.
Plaquette corollary: ‖κ_p‖ ≤ 192·d for general (G, ρ) on the torus.

**§7. The circle instance: κ_p = −1/2 (the evaluated half of clause ii).** For
G = Circle, ρ = circleRep (d = 1), torus N ≥ 2:

- Character orthogonality on (Circle, haarProb Circle) is proved from translation
  invariance alone: ∫ z^k = 0 whenever some w has w^k ≠ 1; consequently ∫ z = ∫ z² =
  ∫ z⁻¹ = ∫ (z²)⁻¹ = 0.
- A plaquette character factors as a product of per-link factors φ (z on the two forward
  links, z⁻¹ on the two backward links, 1 elsewhere); the four links of a torus plaquette
  are pairwise distinct for N ≥ 2.
- The mixed moments vanish by an explicit witness-link case analysis: ∫ W_p·W_q = 0 for
  **every** ordered pair (including q = p), and ∫ W_p·conj(W_q) = 0 for q ≠ p — in each
  case one of q's forward links carries a character (z², z, (z²)⁻¹, or z⁻¹) whose Haar
  integral against p's factor is zero. The two torus-dead configurations are excluded by
  a coordinate argument ((σ_μ(σ_ν x))_μ = x_μ + 1 ≠ x_μ in ZMod N for N ≥ 2).
- Assembly via (Re w : ℂ) = (w + conj w)/2 and ⟨W_p⟩₀ = 0 (the Stage-0 circle instance):
  Cov₀(W_p, D_q) = −1/2 if q = p, else 0. Summing the link-sharing sum:

  **κ_p = Cov₀(W_p, A) = −1/2**, hence **d/dβ ⟨W_p⟩_β |₀ = +1/2**, and with the §3
  constant, ⟨W_p⟩_β = β·(1/2) + O(β²) rigorously at fixed volume
  (`gibbsExpect_circle_plaquette_firstOrder`).
