**Weights, read how.** For a primitive unimodular direction

landed π-general object — the lifted eigenvalue
`(π (u1ParamGen α β t) * piOp (π (u1ParamGen α β t))) s s` —

target is proportionality to the COMMON HYPERCHARGE MATRIX's
vector. Three distinct classification grades, never to be

  weights(π) = x }`. This is the grade forcing actually
  needs: forcing at the base set is exactly the statement
  that Grade W is the single proportionality ray of the
  table — already known FALSE in the toggle subfamily
  (piFlip, piDouble are two off-ray points, T1); Grade W
  asks for *all* of it, including the B−L question (§1.4).
- **Grade T (toggle-orbit classification).** Classify the

  the orbit structure of the realized ones under the swap
  group (§1.3, CLS-2). This is the grade every landed
