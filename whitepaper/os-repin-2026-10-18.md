## §1. Corrections to the old pin (all T1, named as corrections)

The 2026-10-06-era pin (`OsterwalderSchrader.lean`, with its
sibling `WightmanAxioms.lean`; both files **unmodified** by this
brick) is defective in three named ways.

**Correction 1 — the premise is stale.** The old header asserts
"Mathlib (2026) has no tempered distributions," and on that basis
pins Schwinger functions as an opaque `SchwingerMarker`. False
for the pinned Mathlib v4.35.0-rc3: `𝓢'(E, F)` ships with
Fourier theory (`Mathlib/Analysis/Distribution/TemperedDistribution.lean`;
the Pitch 4 scoping §5 audit established this, and Rung 6's
`SchwartzSpace.lean` already builds on it). The marker is
discharged in §2 of the module: Schwinger functions are
`𝓢'(Fin n × Fin 4 → ℝ, ℂ)`. The same stale claim sits in
`WightmanAxioms.lean`'s header and dependency ledger; correcting
that file is its own future re-pin, flagged here, not done here.

**Correction 2 — the old reconstruction statement is vacuous.**
`OSReconstruction : Prop := ∀ _ : OsterwalderSchraderAxioms,
∃ _ : WightmanAxioms, True`, over bundles whose every content
field is an unconstrained `Prop`. Both bundles are inhabited by
the all-`True` instantiation, so the statement is a theorem with
zero analytic content. Machine-checked:
`oldPin_osReconstruction_trivial` (T1, no axioms).

**Correction 3 — the old existence statements are vacuous.**
`InteractingOSMeasure : Prop := ∃ _ : OsterwalderSchraderAxioms,
True` and the sibling `InteractingWightmanQFT : Prop :=
∃ _ : WightmanAxioms, True` are likewise provable from the
all-`True` bundles: `oldPin_interactingOSMeasure_trivial`,
`oldPin_interactingWightmanQFT_trivial` (T1, no axioms). The old
pin's "open problems" therefore did not state the open problems.
This is the sharpest finding of the re-pin: the failure was not
that the statements were unproved, but that they were
**unprovable-to-fail** — no inhabitant of the bundles could have
witnessed anything. The re-pinned forms (§2) separate the two

## §2. The re-pin

- **Data.** `S : (n : ℕ) → SchwingerDist n`,
  `SchwingerDist n := 𝓢'(Fin n × Fin 4 → ℝ, ℂ)` — tempered
  distributions on `ℝ⁴ⁿ`, points pair-indexed (point, coordinate);
  linearly isometric to the corpus's flat `Fin (4n) → ℝ`
  convention. All E1–E4 transformations are written on coordinate
  tuples (`firstBlock`, `secondBlock`, `reflFirst`,
  `euclidMotion`), and the axioms quantify over Schwartz
  functions whose underlying plain function equals a transformed
  one — stating the axioms needs no `𝓢`-composition machinery
  and hides no membership claim.
- **E0 — no field.** Temperedness *is* the type `𝓢'`. Three
  glue theorems make the content explicit:
  `schwingerDist_continuous`, `schwingerDist_add`,
  `schwingerDist_smul` (continuity + linearity of the pairing).
  Contrast the old pin, where `temperedness : Prop` could be
  `False`.
- **E1** (`OSBundle.euclideanCovariant`): invariance under the
  diagonal action of the full orthogonal group `O(4)` (stated
  against the dot product, `IsOrthogonal`) and translations.
- **E2** (`OSBundle.reflectionPositive`): for every finite
  family of test functions supported in the strict positive-time
  half-space (`InPositiveTime`: every point's time coordinate
  positive on the support) and every factorized family
  `G i j = conj(F i ∘ reflFirst) · (F j ∘ secondBlock)`, the sum
  `∑ i j, S (nᵢ + nⱼ) (G i j)` is a nonnegative real.
- **E3** (`OSBundle.symmetric`): invariance under permuting the
  points of the test function.
- **E4** (`OSBundle.clustering`): for a factorized family with
  the second factor translated by `T` in the time direction,
  `S (n+m) (F T) → S n f * S m g` as `T → ∞` (`Filter.Tendsto`,
  `atTop`).
- **Vacuum normalization** is a bundle field:
  `S 0 f = f 0` (the 0-point function is the constant `1`
  distribution).
- **Non-Gaussianity** (`NonGaussian`): Wick-defect form — some
  `f` with `S 2 (f⊗f) ≠ 0` and
  `S 4 (f⊗f⊗f⊗f) ≠ 3·(S 2 (f⊗f))²`, tensor factors pinned by
  plain-function factorization identities.
- **Existence, re-pinned:** `InteractingOSMeasureStatement :=
  ∃ B : OSBundle, NonGaussian B.S`. Unproved. Not claimed.
- **Reconstruction, re-pinned as a schema:**
  `OSReconstructionStatement (IsBoundaryValueOf) (SatisfiesWightman)`
  — see §4 for why a schema is the honest form at this price.

**A nuance the re-pin makes visible (recorded, not a defect):**
E0–E4 plus normalization do **not** exclude the trivial theory
(the zero field: `S₀ = 1`, all higher pairings factoring through
the degenerate solution). In OS theory this is correct — the
axioms admit the trivial theory, and *interacting* content is
exactly the extra clause. The old pin's disease was different
(vacuity of shape); the new pin separates "satisfies OS" from
"is non-Gaussian" cleanly, which is what an existence statement
needs.

## §4. The reconstruction theorem, audited

The old pin listed the analytic gap as four items and asserted
Mathlib "has none of (1)–(3)". Re-audited against the pinned
Mathlib (greps over `Mathlib/` in the pinned checkout,
2026-10-18):

1. **Tempered distributions + Fourier — PRESENT (gap item
   partially closed).** `TemperedDistribution.lean` ships `𝓢'`
   and `TemperedDistribution.fourierTransformCLM` (Fourier
   transform on `𝓢'`). What remains absent on this line: a
   Laplace-transform / boundary-value theory for distributions
   on tube domains (no Laplace file under
   `Mathlib/Analysis/Distribution/`).
2. **Bargmann–Hall–Wightman (analytic continuation to the
   extended tube) — ABSENT.** `grep -ri bargmann Mathlib/`:
   0 hits.
3. **Edge-of-the-wedge — ABSENT.** `grep -ri "edge of the
   wedge" Mathlib/`: 0 hits; no wedge file under
   `Mathlib/Analysis/Complex/`.
4. **Hilbert space from the OS form — machinery exists in the
   abstract (Lp, quotients, completion), corpus instance stands
   at lattice level** via the time-slice route (1b-C). The
   continuum instance is additionally blocked *behind E2 at
   general β* (the Gram clause) before any analysis starts:
   without positivity there is no form to quotient.
5. **Growth control for the limit** (the E0 side of a continuum
   limit): Stage 3a/3c territory, unbuilt.

Why the statement is a **schema**: a reconstruction statement is
exactly as strong as its continuation relation. Pinning
`∃ W, True`-shaped conclusions over real data would repeat the
old pin's disease one level up. `OSReconstructionStatement`
therefore takes the two relations as named parameters —
`IsBoundaryValueOf` (items 2+3 above; the analytic core) and
`SatisfiesWightman` (the sibling pin's own future re-pin) — and
pins everything around them: quantifier shape, types, data.
**Price of filling the holes: research-scale (the gauntlet's
§9-wall territory); no part of it is priced S–M, and none is
implied by this brick.**

**Surprises.**

   package's headlines compose into clause citations with only
   one elaboration friction (the torus extent `N` needs an

   verification path, cost: three annotations).

   non-theorem citation — and it is the honest one: there is no
   lattice permutation-symmetry theorem because the lattice
   Schwinger data are *defined* as expectations of commuting
   products. Inventing a wrapper theorem for it would have
   manufactured a citation, not a fact.
