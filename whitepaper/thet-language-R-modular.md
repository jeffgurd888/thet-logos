## 1. The idea in one paragraph

The language requires R to be a self-adjoint generator of repetition. The
deepest candidate in mathematical physics for "repetition as such" is
**modular flow**: given an algebra of thet events and a thermal (KMS) state,
Tomita–Takesaki theory produces a canonical one-parameter group — time
itself, in the Connes–Rovelli thermal time hypothesis — generated by the
**modular Hamiltonian** R := −β⁻¹log Δ. Repetition is not something that
happens *in* time; repetition *is* time. This is the thet primitive showing
its face.

## 2. Tomita–Takesaki in brief

Let M be a von Neumann algebra (our model of the thet-event algebra) and φ a
faithful KMS state at inverse temperature β — i.e., an equilibrium state for
the flow it will itself define. The GNS construction gives (H_φ, π_φ, Ω_φ).
Define S π(A)Ω = π(A)*Ω; its polar decomposition S = JΔ^{1/2} yields:

- the **modular operator** Δ ≥ 0,
- the **modular flow** σ_t(A) = Δ^{it} A Δ^{−it}, a one-parameter automorphism
  group of M,
- the **KMS condition**: φ(A σ_{iβ}(B)) = φ(BA) — equilibrium with respect to
  the flow.

The generator: σ_t = e^{iRt} with **R = −β⁻¹ log Δ**, the modular
Hamiltonian. (Standard references: Takesaki, *Theory of Operator Algebras
II*; Bratteli–Robinson vol. 1, §5.3; Connes–Rovelli, *Class. Quant. Grav.*
11 (1994) 2899 for thermal time.)

## 4. Finite-dimensional worked model (verified)

M = M_n(ℂ), φ(A) = Tr(ρA), ρ = diag(λ₁,…,λₙ) > 0. Then on the GNS space
ℂⁿ⊗ℂⁿ: Δ|k⟩|l⟩ = (λ_l/λ_k)|k⟩|l⟩, so with E_k = −log λ_k,

$$ R\,|k\rangle|l\rangle = (E_k - E_l)\,|k\rangle|l\rangle, \qquad
   \sigma_t(|k\rangle\langle l|) = e^{i(E_k-E_l)t}\,|k\rangle\langle l|. $$

**Qubit** (ρ = diag(0.7, 0.3)): R spectrum = {0, 0, ±0.8473}; the single
harmonic ω = log(0.7/0.3) ≈ 0.8473. The flow visibly rotates observables:
σ_t(σ_x) = cos(ωt)σ_x + sin(ωt)σ_y, verified to 1.6×10⁻¹⁶.

**Qutrit** (ρ = diag(0.5, 0.3, 0.2)): R spectrum =
{0 (×3), ±0.4055, ±0.5108, ±0.9163} — a **chord** of three incommensurate
harmonics; the flow is quasi-periodic, never exactly repeating. "Chemistry
is harmony" made literal: complex thet-repetition is multi-harmonic.

**KMS verified numerically:** |φ(Aσ_i(B)) − φ(BA)| = 2.2×10⁻¹⁶ (qubit),
4.7×10⁻¹⁶ (qutrit), random A, B.

and fixed during verification (flow direction; complex-time factor) — the
committed script carries the corrected conventions.

## 5. Thet's face: thermal time

The Connes–Rovelli thermal time hypothesis: physical time flow *is* the
modular flow of the statistical state describing the observer's ignorance.
In thet language this becomes an identity, not a hypothesis:

> **Time is the repetition of thet. A clock is a thet process whose modular
> harmonics we count.**

KMS equilibrium — previously downgraded to a "consistency check" in v2 §6 —
is now promoted to its proper place: it is the *definition* of the state
with respect to which R is built. The language does not derive KMS; it
*starts* from a KMS state and reads off R. The honest direction is preserved.

## 6. Relation to the finite triple (honesty)

R_modular = −β⁻¹log Δ and R_triple = D_F are **different operators with
different roles**: one generates state-dependent time flow, the other encodes
fermion masses and mixings as a geometric Dirac operator. This document does
**not** claim they are equal, and does not derive D_F from modular flow.

The open question this instantiation poses — not answers: *is there a KMS
state on the thet algebra whose modular Hamiltonian deforms to, or
recovers, D_F?* If yes, the two instantiations are two faces of one R, and
thet's face is fully shown. If no, the language honestly has (at least) two
models of repetition. Both outcomes are results.

Further honesty:
- The thet-event algebra is *modeled* by M_n(ℂ) here, not constructed as a
  von Neumann algebra from first principles. This is a model, not a
  derivation.
- The KMS state (temperature β, Gibbs weights) is **input**, exactly as the
  Yukawa matrices are input in the triple model. Nothing is smuggled.
- The type-III (QFT) case is cited, not constructed.
