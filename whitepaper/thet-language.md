## 1. Primitives

Three signs. Nothing else is primitive.

- **0** — the void. No distinction, no correspondence.
- **.** — the point. The first distinction, 0 . 0.
- **{ | }** — the correspondence. `{` and `}` are the relata; **`|` is the Action**.

Axiom (primacy of correspondence): there is no `{` `}` without `|`.

*Repair (v2):* v1 told a story about thet "meeting itself" to explain how the
first correspondence happens before any relata exist. That story is circular —
a self presupposes a relatum — and it is hereby withdrawn. The `.` is an
**unanalyzed primitive**: distinction arises; the language does not explain
*how* the first distinction happens, only what follows from it. An honest
primitive beats a dishonest derivation.

## 2. The thet event

$$ e = \{ \; | \; \} $$

One action at the bar. The event is the atom: it has no parts, only its
happening. `{ }` are its traces.

## 3. The repeat operator R (defined, v2)

Let A be the algebra generated by thet events under the compositions of §4,
represented on a Hilbert space H (the GNS construction for a chosen state, or
any concrete model). **R is the self-adjoint operator on H that generates
repetition of the thet action.** Its defining property:

$$ R\,\psi = \lambda\,\psi $$

— the stable repetitions are the eigenmodes, the harmonics, with real
eigenvalues λ (self-adjointness is required precisely so the harmonics are
real and stable).

*Concrete model:* in the finite spectral triple, **R := D_F**, the Dirac
operator on ℂ³² (ℂ⁹⁶ for three generations). Its discrete spectrum is the
harmonic structure; the spectral action sums over it. This is not an analogy —
it is the definition of R in our best current model. Other models may define R
differently; the language requires only self-adjointness and the unfolding
rule of §4.

*Status:* R is now defined per model rather than gestured at. The abstract
language constrains R (self-adjoint, generates repetition); each model
instantiates it.

## 4. The grammar, axiomatized (v2)

Thet events compose. The compositions obey:

- **Sequential** $e_2 \circ e_1$: associative, $(e_3 \circ e_2) \circ e_1 =
  e_3 \circ (e_2 \circ e_1)$, with a unit event $1$ (the silent action:
  $e \circ 1 = 1 \circ e = e$).
- **Parallel** $e_1 \otimes e_2$: associative, unital, symmetric
  ($e_1 \otimes e_2 \cong e_2 \otimes e_1$).
- **Interchange law:** $(e_2 \circ e_1) \otimes (f_2 \circ f_1) =
  (e_2 \otimes f_2) \circ (e_1 \otimes f_1)$ — sequential and parallel
  composition cohere.

*Position (v2):* this grammar is a **process theory** in the sense of
categorical quantum mechanics (Coecke–Abramsky). That literature is prior art
and is acknowledged. What the thet language adds is two axioms standard
process theories do not require: (a) the primacy of correspondence — every
system and process unfolds into `{ | }` events, nothing enters from outside;
(b) the harmonic structure principle (§5) — stable structures are eigenmodes
of repetition. If those additions turn out to be redundant, the language
collapses into existing process theories, and that collapse is itself a
result.

*Greatest freedom:* any finite composition respecting the axioms is
well-formed. *Ontological continuity:* every expression unfolds into thet
events. The axioms are what let freedom and continuity coexist — without them,
v1 had freedom without law.

## 5. The harmonic structure principle (revised)

Stable structures are the eigenmodes of R. A particle, a bound state, a
geometry is a standing pattern in repeated thet-action — the harmonics that
reinforce rather than cancel.

This is now a well-formed claim because R is defined (§3): in the finite
model it says the particle spectrum is the Dirac spectrum, which is exactly
what the spectral triple program computes.

## 7. Quarantined: mass ratios

"Mass ratios are harmonic ratios" ($m_\mu/m_e \approx 206.77$ as a chord) is
**quarantined** as of v2. Without a pre-registered harmonic series, any ratio
can be fit post-hoc — this is the φ-numerology disease and it is not admitted.
Readmission requires: a specific series stated *before* contact with the data,
then a hit. The worked example
(`thet-language-example-mass.md`) is marked accordingly.

## 8. Criticizability (v2)

v1 contained a sentence ("to express thet is to participate") that shielded
the language from outside critique. It is removed. The language is criticizable
from outside; using it exempts nothing. If the primacy axiom or the harmonic
principle is wrong, show it — the kill criteria below say how.

## 9. Kill criteria

- **K1.** If R cannot be instantiated beyond the finite triple — no second
  model within the next research push — the language is a redescription of one
  model, not a language. Downgrade to notation.
- **K2.** If the mass-ratio program produces only post-hoc fits, it stays
  quarantined permanently. No readmission without pre-registration.
- **K3.** If no composition theorem (an order-one analog for composed thet
  events) can even be *stated* precisely, v3 does not ship.
