## 1. What was built

One exact factor-2 blocking step for the lattice gauge theory of
Stages 0–1b, in the corpus's abstract-first form: a `BlockingScheme`
(§1 of the module) packages the data — an injective embedding
`ι : Λ' → Λ` of coarse sites into fine sites with the two parity
axioms (two fine steps from a coarse site are one coarse step;
one fine step never lands on a coarse site). The concrete torus
instance (extent `2M → M`, coordinate doubling) is §9. Blocked link
variables are path-ordered products of the two fine links along each
blocked edge (`blockMap`); the blocked measure is the pushforward
(`blockedMeasure`, `blockedGibbsMeasure`).

## 2. The spine, letter by letter

**(a) Blocking defined — STANDS.** `BlockingScheme`, `fineLinkA/B`
(the two fine links of a blocked edge, proved distinct and
injective in each slot), `blockMap`. On the torus (§9):
`torusBlocking`, with `twoStep` proved through the wrap-around case
(coordinate `M − 1`: the fine side gives `2M ≡ 0`, the coarse side
gives `double(0) = 0`) and `oddStep` proved by parity of `ZMod.val`
(doubled values are even; one fine step makes them odd).

**(b) Covariance and measure invariance — STANDS.**
`blockMap_gaugeExtend`: blocked variables are gauge-covariant for
the coarse gauge action lifted to the fine lattice by `gaugeExtend`
(coarse gauge values at coarse sites, `1` elsewhere — the lift is
*chosen*, since a coarse gauge transformation does not determine a
fine one; covariance is proved for this canonical lift).
`blockedGibbsMeasure_map_gaugeAction`: the blocked Gibbs measure is
gauge invariant in the strong `Measure.map` form at **every real β**
(transported from Stage 1a's `gibbsMeasure_map_gaugeAction`, which is
itself the Measure.map strengthening proved in Stage 1b-A's §6
pattern). The blocked measure is a probability measure at every β.

**(c) Exact β = 0 statements — STANDS, in the strongest form.**
`blockedMeasure_configMeasure`: the pushforward of the fine product
Haar measure under blocking **is** the coarse product Haar measure
(blocked links are pairwise products of independent Haar variables;
proved by a rectangle π-system argument and Mathlib's
`measurePreserving_pi` for the pairwise multiplication map).
Consequently `blockedGibbsMeasure_zero`: the blocked Gibbs measure
at β = 0 **is** the coarse Gibbs measure at β = 0 — the blocked
theory at β = 0 is not merely equivalent to but *equal to* the
coarse Stage-0 theory at β = 0, so the whole β = 0 package
transports verbatim (Haar-intertwiner expectations, the vanishing
criterion, Stage 1a's disjoint-support factorization for blocked
observables). The plaquette case is proved explicitly:
`gibbsExpect_zero_blocked_plaquette` (expectation = `tr P`, the Haar
intertwiner trace) and `gibbsExpect_zero_blocked_plaquette_eq_zero`
(the expectation vanishes under Stage 0's no-nonzero-fixed-vectors
hypothesis, transported verbatim). Fixed points named and
proved: product Haar is a fixed point of the blocking pushforward;
the trivial configuration's Dirac measure is also one
(`blockedMeasure_dirac_one`).

**(d) Effective action and what does not survive — STANDS as
specified (existence proved; Wilson form fenced).** §8 proves the
blocked Gibbs measure and the coarse product Haar measure are
**mutually absolutely continuous** at every β (both densities are
exponentials, hence strictly positive; transport through `blockMap`
by `Measure.AbsolutelyContinuous.map`). Therefore the blocked
measure has an exact effective action: `blockedAction` = minus the
log of its Radon–Nikodym density `blockedDensity` against the
coarse product Haar measure, and
`blockedGibbsMeasure_eq_withDensity_exp` proves the blocked measure
is *exactly* the coarse configuration measure weighted by
`exp (−S_eff)` (the density is nonzero and finite a.e. — the zero
set is killed by mutual absolute continuity). What is **not**
claimed: that `S_eff` is of single-plaquette Wilson form at β > 0.
That statement is fenced as the named proposition
`BlockedGibbsIsCoarseGibbs B β β'` in the module, **proved at
β = 0** (`blockedGibbsIsCoarseGibbs_zero`, with β′ = 0) and open at
β > 0 — it is a Stage-3 question. Supporting evidence for why the
fence sits exactly there is itself proved: see §3 below.

**Composition — STANDS.** Two blockings compose: as functions,
`blockMap ∘ blockMap = block4Map` (the four-link product map, with
the `((a*b)*(c*d))` grouping), and as measures,
`blockedMeasure_blockedMeasure`. Torus chain `2(2K) → 2K → K`
stated explicitly. Composition is proved via function composition
and `Measure.map_map`, not via kernel Fubini (the scheme here is
deterministic — see deviations).

## 3. The constraint-closure probe — the answer

The commission asked: does any proved constraint/bound from
Stages 0–1b survive **verbatim** under the blocked map? Answer,
item by item, all proved in this module:

| Constraint (source) | Survives blocking? |
|---|---|
| Gauge invariance of the Gibbs measure, strong `Measure.map` form, every β (1a/1b-A) | **YES** — `blockedGibbsMeasure_map_gaugeAction` |
| The β = 0 theory itself (Stage 0 package: intertwiner expectations, vanishing criterion; 1a factorization) | **YES, verbatim** — the blocked β = 0 theory *equals* the coarse β = 0 theory (`blockedGibbsMeasure_zero`) |
| Elitzur: vanishing of open-link expectations (1a) | **YES, at every β**, for blocked links — `expectMatrix_blocked_singleLink_eq_zero`: under the fine Gibbs measure, the expectation matrix of a blocked link vanishes provided ρ has no nonzero fixed vectors (the same fixed-vector hypothesis as 1a); torus instance `torus_blocked_elitzur` |
| Wilson loops as well-defined observables | **YES** — a coarse Wilson loop is *equal* to the fine Wilson loop over the doubled path (`wilsonLoop_blocked_plaquette`, via `doubleStep`/`loopProd` induction, both orientations) |
| Single-link / open fine observables | **NO — proved boundary.** `singleLink_not_factor_blockMap`: a fine single-link observable does **not** factor through the blocking map (two fine configurations with the same blocked image — constant `1`, and `c`/`c⁻¹` on one blocked pair — take different values on it). Fine single-link data is exactly what blocking integrates out; its expectations are not determined by blocked data |
| Single-plaquette Wilson form of the action at β > 0 | **NOT CLAIMED** — fenced (`BlockedGibbsIsCoarseGibbs`, §8/§2(d)); the effective action exists exactly as a log-density |

Reading of the probe (this is the dossier's interpretation, labeled
as such): the constraint set {gauge invariance, β = 0 exactness,
Elitzur, loop factorization} is **closed under one exact blocking
step** — proved, not assumed — and §7 gives the induction skeleton
(composition) that would carry it across scales. The probe also
marks the set's edge precisely: everything in the set is a
*qualitative* (exact/structural) constraint. No constraint in
Stages 0–1b is a *quantitative uniform* bound (on a gap or a
correlation length), and blocking does not manufacture one: the
uniform estimate remains Stage 3c, untouched, exactly as priced.
The constraint-closure strategy is thereby confirmed as *testable
and so far true on the qualitative constraints*, and its missing
ingredient is located in the same place as before — a uniform
quantitative constraint to add to the closed set.

## 5. Deviations from the spine, named

1. **Abstract-first, torus as instance.** The spine was phrased on
   the concrete torus; following the corpus's established pattern
   (Stages 0–1b are abstract with torus instances), the step is
   built for an abstract `BlockingScheme` and the torus is §9. The
   torus content the spine asked for is all present and proved
   (twoStep through the wrap, oddStep by parity, β = 0 and Elitzur
   and plaquette corollaries, composition chain).
2. **Deterministic blocking kernel.** The "partial-integration"
   blocked measure is realized as the pushforward under the
   deterministic blocking map (a delta kernel). Integrating out
   fine fluctuations with a nontrivial kernel is a different,
   larger construction; nothing in the spine's exact statements
   requires it, and the pushforward form is what makes the β = 0
   and invariance statements exact equalities rather than
   equivalences. Flagged here so the choice is on the record.
3. **Elitzur route.** The transport is proved on the blocked side
   (from §3's blocked-measure gauge invariance plus the 1a
   fixed-vector argument applied to the blocked Gibbs measure);
   an earlier plan to pull a fine-side covariance identity through
   the two-link path fails as a proof strategy (a two-link path
   under a single-site fine update transforms with mixed
   source/target factors, not by the left multiplication the
   argument needs) — the blocked-side route proves the stated
   theorem, and the dead end is recorded so it is not retried.
4. **`gaugeExtend` is a choice.** Coarse gauge covariance is
   proved for the canonical lift (coarse values at coarse sites,
   `1` at odd sites). A general fine gauge transformation does not
   preserve blocked variables in any simpler form, and none is
   claimed.
