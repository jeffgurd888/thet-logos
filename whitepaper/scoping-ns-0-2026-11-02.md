2. A named divergence operator `div u = ∑ i, ∂ᵢ uᵢ` (from `fderiv` /

3. The NS system as a Prop: equation (1) pointwise —
   `∂t uᵢ + ∑ⱼ uⱼ ∂ⱼ uᵢ = ν Δuᵢ − ∂ᵢ p + fᵢ` — plus (2) `div u = 0` and
   (3) `u(x,0) = u°(x)`, with the convective term `(u·∇)u` packaged.
4. Smoothness/global-existence quantifiers: `u, p ∈ C^∞(ℝ³ × [0,∞))`
   (Fefferman (6); periodic analogue (10)) — statable via `ContDiff`, but
   the half-line domain `[0,∞)` and joint space-time smoothness must be
   encoded deliberately, not assumed.
5. The finite-energy side condition (7): `∃ C, ∀ t ≥ 0, ∫_{ℝ³} ‖u(x,t)‖² dx < C`
   (periodic analogue: integral over ℝ³/ℤ³, condition under (11)).
6. Periodicity package for (B)/(D): (8) `u°(x+eⱼ) = u°(x)`,
   `f(x+eⱼ,t) = f(x,t)`; (11) `u(x+eⱼ,t) = u(x,t)` — **plus the errata

7. Breakdown Props in Fefferman's quantifier shape (§3): C/D are
   *existence of admissible (u°, f) for which NO (p,u) satisfies
   (1),(2),(3),(6),(7)* (resp. (10),(11)) — a ¬∃ statement over smooth
   finite-energy solutions, **not** "∃ a blowing-up weak solution."

## 3. NS-1 SHAPE — the four alternatives (Fefferman, Clay text)

Source: Charles L. Fefferman, *"Existence and Smoothness of the
Navier–Stokes Equation,"* CMI official problem description
(claymath.org PDF, fetched 2026-11-02). "We ask for a proof of one of the
following four statements" — **any ONE of A–D, proved, solves the problem.**
The board statement is their disjunction: **ClayNS := A ∨ B ∨ C ∨ D** in the
Clay text's exact senses — NOT a single existence claim.

- **(A) Existence and smoothness on ℝ³.** ν > 0, n = 3. For **any** smooth,
  divergence-free u° satisfying **(4)**
  `|∂^α u°(x)| ≤ C_{αK}(1+|x|)^{−K}` on ℝ³ for all α, K; **f ≡ 0**.
  Then there exist smooth p, u on ℝ³×[0,∞) satisfying (1), (2), (3), **(6)**
  (p, u ∈ C^∞(ℝ³×[0,∞))) and **(7)** (bounded energy:
  `∫ ‖u(x,t)‖² dx < C` for all t ≥ 0).
- **(B) Existence and smoothness on ℝ³/ℤ³.** ν > 0, n = 3. For any smooth,
  divergence-free u° satisfying **(8)** (period-1 in each eⱼ; pressure
  periodicity per the errata); **f ≡ 0**. Then there exist smooth p, u on
  ℝ³×[0,∞) satisfying (1), (2), (3), **(10)** (u periodic) and **(11)**
  (p, u ∈ C^∞).
- **(C) Breakdown on ℝ³.** ν > 0, n = 3. There **exist** a smooth,
  divergence-free u° on ℝ³ and a smooth f on ℝ³×[0,∞) satisfying **(4)** and
  **(5)** `|∂^α ∂t^m f(x,t)| ≤ C_{αmK}(1+|x|+t)^{−K}` for all α, m, K,
  **for which there exist no solutions (p,u)** of (1), (2), (3), (6), (7)
  on ℝ³×[0,∞).
- **(D) Breakdown on ℝ³/ℤ³.** ν > 0, n = 3. There exist a smooth,
  divergence-free u° on ℝ³ and a smooth f on ℝ³×[0,∞) satisfying **(8)**
  and **(9)** (`|∂^α ∂t^m f(x,t)| ≤ C_{αmK}(1+|t|)^{−K}` on ℝ³×[0,∞) for
  all α, m, K), for which there exist no solutions (p,u) of (1), (2), (3),
  (10), (11) on ℝ³×[0,∞).

Honesty clauses on the face: A is **not** the negation of C (C permits a
chosen smooth force; A fixes f ≡ 0); likewise B/D. ¬A ⇒ C and ¬C ⇒ A hold;
no reverse implications. A proof of C/D leaves A/B undecided.

### K-NS-1 — ENCODING FENCE (side conditions the pin MUST carry verbatim)

Any encoding weaker than the Clay text on any of these is vacuous — kill
and re-enter:

1. **(4) initial-data decay, all α and all K** (Schwartz-grade), for A/C;
   smoothness + divergence-free u° in **all four**.
2. **(5)/(9) forcing decay, all α, m, K** — (5) in (1+|x|+t), (9) in
   (1+|t|) on the torus — for C/D; **f ≡ 0 exactly** for A/B (no "small"
   or "decaying" force substitute).
3. **(6)/(10)+(11) genuine C^∞ smoothness** of (u, p) on the full
   half-line `[0,∞)` — encoding weak, Sobolev, or local-in-time solutions
   where the text says smooth is the registered K-NS-1 kill (registration
   §3.4, verbatim: "a pin that encodes weak solutions where the statement
   says smooth, or drops a decay/forcing hypothesis, mis-states the
   problem — kill and re-enter").
4. **(7) global bounded energy** `∫ ‖u‖² < C` uniform over all t ≥ 0 —
   dropping it (or making C depend on t) admits trivial "solutions" and
   voids the alternatives.
5. **(8) periodicity including the errata pressure clause** for B/D.
6. **C/D quantifier shape**: ∃ admissible (u°, f) with ¬∃ smooth
   finite-energy (p, u) — not an exhibited singular weak solution.
7. **ν > 0 and n = 3** fixed on the face of every alternative.

The October 2026 fidelity critique (arXiv:2610.08144, §1.3) is the standing
cautionary exhibit for this fence: a formalisation can check cleanly while
stating something other than its NL source. NS-1's defence is clause-by-
clause carriage of (4)–(11) above, on the face of the pin.
