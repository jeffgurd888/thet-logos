## 2. The route as built

**β1.** Take `z` in the adjoint domain and `y = H† z`. The only property of
the pair ever used is the pairing identity at basis vectors, which is
Mathlib's `LinearPMap.adjoint_isFormalAdjoint` fed with the corpus's
unconditional `dom_dense`. Fix a configuration `c₀`, `n := tot c₀`. Then
`coefB z c₀ = conj ⟪y, H e_{c₀}⟫`. Expanding `H e_{c₀}` in coordinates, every
term with `tot γ ≠ n` vanishes — `H e_{c₀}` lies in sector `n` because the
balanced quartic preserves sectors *exactly*
(`hamIntBos_mem_domSector`, Δn = 0 — ESA-α's band correction) — so the
pairing is a sum over the single sector `sectorFinset d n`. On the other
side, the coordinates of `H (truncSector y n)` at `c₀` expand by linearity,
and the matrix-element symmetry `(H e_γ)_{c₀} = conj (H e_{c₀})_γ` is plain
symmetry of `H` on the finite-particle domain (`hamIntBos_isSymmetric`).
The two sector sums agree term by term. No direct-sum adjoint API, no
sectorwise-adjoint theorem, and no "dense subclass" restriction were needed:
the characterization is for the whole adjoint domain at once.

**β2.** A coordinate of `H (truncVec y N)` at `c₀`: if `tot c₀ ≤ N`, exact
sector preservation kills every summand outside sector `tot c₀`, the sum
collapses to the β1 sector sum, and the coordinate is `coefB z c₀`; if
`tot c₀ > N`, every summand vanishes and so does the coordinate of
`truncVec z N`. Hence `H (truncVec y N) = truncVec z N` *exactly*, for every
`N` — the "graph-norm tail estimate" priced in α does not exist as a
separate step, because the operator identity is exact at finite cutoff.
Convergence is then just the basis expansion: `truncVec y N → y` and
`truncVec z N → z` by `lp.hasSum_single` composed with the cofinality of
`totLeFinset`.

**β3.** An adjoint graph pair `(y, z)` is the limit of the `hamPMap` graph
pairs `(truncVec y N, truncVec z N)`, hence lies in the closure of the graph
(`mem_closure_iff_seq_limit`), which is the graph of the closure by
`hamPMap_isClosable` + `IsClosable.graph_closure_eq_closure_graph`. So
`(hamPMap)† ≤ (hamPMap).closure` by `LinearPMap.le_of_le_graph`, and ESA-α's
equivalence closes `BosonicESA`. Von Neumann's double-adjoint identity is
not used (it remains absent from the pinned Mathlib and remains fenced in
α — it is simply not on this route).

  the graph norm (free-part `(n+1)`-weights, interaction terms via relative
  bounds). As α's own band correction anticipated, exact sector

  unconditional theorems, so the result is the full ∀ `d lam W g`

## 5. Scope and fences

- **Finite modes only.** `Dom d` is the finite-particle domain over `Fin d`
  modes; the Fock space is `ℓ²` over the countable configuration set of a
  *finite* mode set. Nothing here constructs or controls an infinite-mode or
  continuum theory.
- **ESA ≠ gap.** This module proves essential self-adjointness of the
  finite-mode bosonic Hamiltonian. It does not revive uniform gap
  persistence (disproved in-module in Campaign 2), and it does not touch
  the Schrödinger/Dyson remainders or any Pitch 4 clause.
- The fenced von Neumann form in ESA-α (`T†† = T.closure`, absent from the
  pinned Mathlib) stays fenced and stays unproved; β did not need it.
