## The correction (first, per house rule)

The columns are oriented edges whose directions lie
only in {+e1, +e2, +e3} (the direction type is
{ν : Fin 4 // ν ≠ 0} — three values). A genuine
reverse column over the same unordered site pair
would need a −eᵢ direction; it exists only when
N ∣ 2, where +eᵢ wraps onto its own reverse. At
3 ≤ N, the previously landed `colSites_eq_imp`
forces **multiplicity ≤ 1** over every unordered
site pair (proved here as
`colSites_fiber_card_le_one`, over the general
bound ≤ 2 at any even N). Therefore:

- **Digon pairs do not exist at 3 ≤ N** — and with
  them the digon⊔digon, (3,3,2), and (4,2,2)
  patterns are all **uninhabited** there.
- **Sharp form (`primitive_pattern_eq_C4`):** at
  (Even N, 3 ≤ N), *every* primitive quad has
  pattern C4 — a single 4-cycle of columns.
- The PW-2 digon-pair evaluation, landed earlier
  today, covers a family that no primitive quad
  realizes at the assembly's N; it stands as proved
  mathematics about that family (and at N = 2, where
  all four patterns are live), and the record now
  says which. It also explains, after the fact, the
  zero margins at the probe seats.
- **PW-4 is cancelled as priced.** The hub brick
  (L, 40–70) was priced to evaluate the (3,3,2) and
  (4,2,2) hub sites; at the assembly's N those cases
  are empty. The remaining path to `phi_diag_nonneg`
  shortens by a full L brick: PQ-0 → PW-5 (assembly
  over C4 quads only, where every site is degree 2
  and every site integral is a pair twirl — mixed by
  PW-1, same-orientation by PW-3, both evaluated).
