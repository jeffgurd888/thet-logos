## BREAK 2 — Axiom-footprint claim over-extended to CausalProduct theorems

**Location:** `whitepaper/machine-verified-theory-2026-10-10.md`, §4:
"All `#print axioms` on new theorems: `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`
(per Rung 3 verdict dossier)."

**Evidence:** The Rung 3 dossier's original claim
(`whitepaper/rung-3-verdicts-2026-10-10.md:103`) is scoped to Rung 3's new
theorems (ProductTriple.lean J/Krein/FOC — verified Mathlib-only imports;
claim TRUE there). The publication draft extends it corpus-wide, but two
theorems in its own §4 tier table depend on custom axioms:
- `trace_collapse_hypothesis_fails` (`lean/ThetLogos/CausalProduct.lean:220`)
- `product_not_forced_central` (`lean/ThetLogos/CausalProduct.lean:242`)

Both proofs directly invoke file-level axioms: `prod_nonzero_causal_commutator`
(`obtain` at :224, :246), `prodSmul_comp`, `prodSmul_one`, `prodSmul_zero_op`
(:228–232), plus the `prodOpRing` instance. Their `#print axioms` footprint
necessarily includes `ThetLogos.prod_nonzero_causal_commutator` et al. —
strictly wider than `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

**Assessment:** The proofs are genuine (zero sorrys, real derivations). The
failure is tier/footprint labeling: these are T1-proved *consequences of the
T2-axiomatized product structure*, and the draft lists them as plain T1 under
a footprint claim they violate.
**Fix:** scope the footprint claim to the Mathlib-transitive-clean files;
label the two CausalProduct theorems "T1 proved from T2 product axioms
(footprint includes `CausalProduct` axioms)."
