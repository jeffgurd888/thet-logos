first seat. Kill **K-S2-1(a) fired on `𝔸⁺`**: at the U(1) seat
(`N = 4`), the plane-supported observable `F = z − z⁻¹` on the plane

a plane-supported observable is read by its reflected factor at the
*same coordinate, inverted* — the OS integrand is `F²`, not `|F|²`. The
obstruction is therefore **β-independent**: no closed-half positivity

the test of §4. In the same build, **P-0 was confirmed verbatim on the

`Ĥ₀ = 0`, general `(G, ρ)`, T1), and the gate's factorization argument

## 3. (b) The revised gate for S2-2

### 3.1 The algebra, fixed (revised): `𝔸 := 𝔸_slice`

The reconstruction algebra of the ladder is the **slice-generated
algebra**, in the exact form the gate's §1 already defined and S2-1
built on:

```
𝔸_slice = the algebra generated by the slice observables
          F_φ U = φ (restrictConfig sliceLinks U),
```

for bounded measurable `φ` on the time-`0` slice configuration space
`(↥sliceLinks → G)`, with pointwise operations and unit `1`. Its landed
seats, which fix what "landed" means for this definition: the depth-one
identification `osForm_depthOne_transfer` and its β=0 companion
(`LatticeTransferID`); the polarized pair identity
`osForm_depthOne_transfer_pair`, the depth identification
`osForm_depthM`, and `twoPointOp` (R3, `LatticeTransferIdent`); and the
β=0 package of `LatticeReconZero` (§3.7). Membership side conditions
are the landed ones (the `MemLp` conditions S1's and S2-1's statements
carry at their seats); no brick quietly widens the class (gate row 5).

**Why this algebra, stated once, without varnish.** `𝔸_slice` sees
only the time-`0` slice links. The plane stratum (time links at base
times `N/2−1` and `N−1`) is disjoint from `sliceLinks`, so no slice
observable sees the plane coordinate at all — the algebra passes §4's
plane test *by exclusion*, permanently and at every `β`, because the
exclusion is set-theoretic, not integral. It is also the algebra on
which the corpus's transfer machinery (S1, R3) is landed, and the
algebra on which P-0 is a theorem. What the ladder gives up, on the
record: the half-lattice generality the charter hoped for. The
reconstruction is a **slice reconstruction**; the half-lattice theory
is not reconstructed by this ladder, and any label that suggests
otherwise is mis-stated. (The charter's §1 phrase "half-lattice
observables" for `𝔸` is superseded by this revision wherever the two
differ.)

### 3.2 The strict companion seat — what it is for

`osForm₀ (lift χ) (lift φ) = conj(strictState χ) · strictState φ`,
where `strictState` is expectation under the strict half's product Haar
measure; null space `= ker strictState`; `strictState` surjective with
`strictState 1 = 1`, so the quotient is `ℂ·Ω₀` there too;
`strict_transferHat_pairing` — the identity operator carries the OS
pairing on strict classes.

The strict seat is **not** a second domain of the engine. It serves
three named purposes and no others:

1. **Second regression surface.** It is the seat where the gate's
   original factorization argument (`pairing_through_bdry` at

### 3.3 The pairings, re-derived in S1-consistent form

The gate's error, corrected: it defined the engine's pairings on
*representatives* (configuration-space transports of observables). S2-1
proved that route dead for the two-step pairing and unnecessary for the
one-step pairing — S1 had already landed the one-step pairing in
*descended* form. The revision therefore defines both pairings by
factoring through slice states and `transferOp`, the only medium in
which descent is even statable.

**The one-step pairing `Π₁`** (the OS pairing on `𝔸_slice`, in
descended form). For slice observables `F_φ, F_χ ∈ 𝔸_slice`:

```
Π₁(φ, χ) := ⟪sliceState β φ, transferOp (sliceState β χ)⟫_{L²(sliceMeasure)}.
```

This is not a new pairing: by S1's polarized identity
`osForm_depthOne_transfer_pair` (landed, general `(G, ρ)`, every real
`β`), `Π₁(φ, χ) = osForm F_φ F_χ`. The revision's content is that the
engine must *work with the left-hand side* — the form in which the
pairing already reads classes, not representatives.

**The two-step pairing `Π₂`**. For slice observables `F_φ, F_χ`:

```
Π₂(φ, χ) := ⟪sliceState β φ, transferOp (transferOp (sliceState β χ))⟫_{L²(sliceMeasure)}.
```

Defined by **iteration of the landed operator on slice states** — two
applications of `transferOp`, nothing else. Two fences on the
definition's face: (i) it quantifies over `sliceState` images of named
slice observables only — no statement here or downstream asserts
anything about `transferOp` on arbitrary `L²` vectors (the TM R2
fence, gate row 9); (ii) R3's `twoPointOp 2` is the named *candidate*
for the identified form of this iteration, and the identification
`twoPointOp m = transferOp^m` for `m ≥ 2` is **not landed** — the
engine does not need it and must not consume it; `Π₂` is defined by
the iteration itself. Diagonal at `β = 0`: `Π₂(φ, φ)` is the quantity
H-pos(2)ʳ (§3.4) is about.

**Descent — proof-sketch, with the open step named.** Claim: under
H-pos(1)ʳ, both pairings descend to the quotient
`𝔸_slice/𝔑_slice`, `𝔑_slice = {F : Π₁(F, F) = 0}`.

- *`Π₁` descends by construction.* The quotient is by `Π₁`'s own null
  space; under H-pos(1)ʳ, `Π₁` is a positive sesquilinear form,
  Cauchy–Schwarz holds, and null space = radical: `Π₁(φ, χ) = 0`
  whenever `φ` or `χ` is null. This step is complete, not a sketch.
- *`Π₂` at `β = 0` descends by computation.* At `β = 0`,
  `transferOp` is the landed rank-one projection
  `T₀ = ⟪Ω, ·⟫ • Ω` with `⟪Ω, Ω⟫ = 1`, hence idempotent:
  `T₀ ∘ T₀ = T₀`. Therefore `Π₂ = Π₁` at `β = 0`, and `Π₂`'s descent
  at `β = 0` *is* S2-1's landed descent (`osForm_zero_slice_pair`,
  `transferHatZero_pairing`). Moreover the operator the pairing
  characterizes at `β = 0` is forced: `⟨[G], T̂₀[F]⟩ = Π₂(G, F) =
  Π₁(G, F) = ⟨[G], [F]⟩` for all `G`, so `T̂₀ = id` — S2-1's T1
  value, recovered from the pairing's definition, which is what
  §3.7's acceptance test checks.
- *`Π₂` at general `β` — the sketch, and its named gap.* Hermiticity
  first: `Π₂(φ, χ) = conj(Π₂(χ, φ))` follows from `transferOp`'s
  symmetry consumed through S1's polarized identity (the transfer
  form's Hermiticity is `osForm`'s reflection symmetry). Under
  H-pos(2)ʳ, `Π₂` is then a positive sesquilinear form on
  `𝔸_slice`, so Cauchy–Schwarz for `Π₂` makes it vanish on its own
  radical. Descent is therefore exactly the single inclusion
  `rad(Π₁) ⊆ rad(Π₂)`, and the route is: `Π₂` is, by R3's depth
  identification (`osForm_depthM`), the form the **depth-two
  reflected pairing of observables** induces on slice observables —
  an observable pairing again, not a representative pairing — so the
  old gate's (D-desc) route shape applies to it: Cauchy–Schwarz for
  `Π₁` plus measure-invariance of the depth-two transport bounds
  `|Π₂(φ, χ)|² ≤ Π₁(φ, φ) · (depth-two diagonal of χ)`, and a
  `Π₁`-null `φ` forces `Π₂(φ, χ) = 0` for every `χ`. **The domination
  step in that route is a discharge claim, (D-desc)ʳ (§3.5), not a
  landed fact** — the difference from the dead `P₂` is that there the
  failure was a *theorem* (the pairing provably read the wrong norm),
  while here the route is open, named, and kill-guarded
  (K-S2-2(c)ʳ). A gate that presented this step as proved would be
  repeating row 21's mistake.

### 3.4 The hypotheses, restated against the new pairings

**H-pos(1)ʳ — positivity of the one-step form on `𝔸_slice`.**

> For the fixed `(G, ρ, μG)`, every even `N` in scope, and the given
> `β ≥ 0`: for every slice observable `F_φ ∈ 𝔸_slice`, the diagonal
> `Π₁(φ, φ) = osForm F_φ F_φ = ⟪sliceState β φ, transferOp (sliceState β φ)⟫`
> is a nonnegative real number.

**What it buys, exactly:** `Π₁` is a positive sesquilinear form on
`𝔸_slice`; Cauchy–Schwarz; `𝔑_slice` is a linear subspace (the
radical); the quotient `𝔸_slice/𝔑_slice` is a pre-Hilbert space with
`⟨[F_φ], [F_χ]⟩ = Π₁(φ, χ)`; `Ω := [1]` is a unit vector
(`⟨Ω, Ω⟩ = ω(1) = 1`), cyclic by construction; and `Π₂`'s descent
question (§3.3) is well-posed. **What it does not buy (updated):** any
operator — no `T̂`, no boundedness, nothing about `Π₂`. And, new
clause forced by the kill: **nothing outside `𝔸_slice`.** In
particular it asserts no positivity on `𝔸⁺` or on any plane-seeing
class, where the corresponding statement is not unproved but
*refuted at `β = 0`* (row 11); it asserts no positivity on the strict
seat at `β > 0` (at `β = 0` that is T1 by S2-1 §2 — a landed fact about
a companion seat, not a consequence of this hypothesis); and it is
**already discharged at `β = 0`** on `𝔸_slice` by S2-1, so its content
is entirely `β > 0`.

**H-pos(2)ʳ — positivity of the two-step pairing `Π₂`.**

> For the same fixed data and `β ≥ 0`: for every slice observable
> `F_φ ∈ 𝔸_slice`, the two-step diagonal
> `Π₂(φ, φ) = ⟪sliceState β φ, transferOp (transferOp (sliceState β φ))⟫`
> is a nonnegative real number.

**What it buys, exactly:** given H-pos(1)ʳ and the descent claims of
§3.5, the operator `T̂` characterized by
`⟨[F_χ], T̂[F_φ]⟩ = Π₂(χ, φ)` satisfies
`⟨[F], T̂[F]⟩ = Π₂(F, F) ≥ 0` for every `F` — **`T̂ ≥ 0` on `H_OS`**,
from OS positivity twice, as the fence requires. **What it does not
buy (updated):** the space (H-pos(1)ʳ's), `T̂`'s existence or
boundedness (the descent claims'), self-adjointness ((D-symm)ʳ's), or
any statement at any other `β` or on any other algebra. It does
**not** assert positivity of `transferOp`, or of its iterate, on
arbitrary `L²(sliceMeasure)` vectors — its quantifier runs over
`sliceState` images of named slice observables, and any downstream
statement that drops that quantifier has left this gate (TM R2 fence).
And: its `β = 0` instance is **already T1** (at `β = 0`,
`Π₂ = Π₁` by idempotence, and `Π₁`'s diagonal is `|coord φ|² ≥ 0` by
S2-1) — the hypothesis's live content is `β > 0`, and a build that
"verifies" H-pos(2)ʳ only at `β = 0` has verified nothing. The charter
names H-pos(2) the package's pressure point; the revision repeats it
for the new pairing: **any statement of the package that omits
H-pos(2)ʳ is mis-stated.**

The two hypotheses remain **separate**; neither implies the other is
asserted anywhere in this ladder; both are named on the face of every
headline that uses them.

## 4. (c) The plane test — a standing gate

S2-1's obstruction is promoted from a kill report to a **standing
gate**, binding on every future proposal in this program — any
algebra, any pairing, any brick, any item — that touches OS positivity
or reconstruction. No proposal is reviewed until it has taken the
test, on its face.

**The witness (S2-1's shape, fixed).** Let `ℓ₀` be a **plane link**: a
time link at base time `N/2 − 1` — fixed by `reflLink`, lying *inside*
the closed positive half. The smallest seat is the computed one:
`G = U(1)` (the corpus's `Circle`), `μG = haarProb`,
`ρ = circleRep`, `N = 4`. Let `F` be the **plane-supported
observable**

```
F = halfLift of f,   f(y) = (y ℓ₀ : ℂ) − ((y ℓ₀)⁻¹ : ℂ),
```

i.e. `z − z⁻¹` read on the plane coordinate `ℓ₀` and on no other
coordinate. The control values, landed T1: `ω₀(F) = 0`
(`planeObs_state_zero`) and

```
osForm₀ F F = −2        (osForm_planeObs_diag, exact),
```

because `reflConfig` inverts the plane coordinate, so the reflected
factor reads `F` at the *same coordinate, inverted*: the integrand is
`F² = w² − 2 + w⁻²`, and the character integrals `∫ w^{±2}` vanish
over Haar.

**The test.** A proposed algebra `𝔸′` (with a proposed OS pairing)
**passes** iff one of the following holds, exhibited — not asserted:

- **(Exclusion)** `F ∉ 𝔸′` *structurally*: no observable of `𝔸′`
  depends on any plane coordinate. The pass is by the algebra's
  definition (a set-theoretic disjointness, e.g. `𝔸_slice`:
  `sliceLinks` is disjoint from the plane stratum; the strict class:
  functions of `restrictPos` are plane-independent), and it is
  **permanent at every `β`**, because it does not pass through an
  integral.
- **(Computation)** `F ∈ 𝔸′`, and the proposed pairing's diagonal on
  `F` at `β = 0` is computed to be a nonnegative real, **and** the
  computation exhibits why the integrand is not the `F²`-shape above
  (a pairing that reads a plane observable at its own inverted
  coordinate must show what in its definition changes the reading).

**Force at `β > 0`.** The obstruction lives in `reflConfig`'s
definition, so the test is β-independent in force. A pass by exclusion
needs no renewal. A pass by computation is a pass **at `β = 0` only**:
before any positivity statement quantifies over a plane-seeing class
at some `β > 0`, the test is re-run at that `β` — the Gibbs weight
changes the integral, and the fence it has to clear is now explicit
and machine-stated. A proposal that cannot state whether `F ∈ 𝔸′`
has not taken the test. A proposal whose positivity headline
quantifies over a plane-seeing class without a current pass meets
kill K-S2-2(f)ʳ (§6) — and the same standard applies, by this gate's
standing instruction, to any *future* ladder or item that proposes a
positivity-bearing algebra: **the plane test is cleared before the
positivity argument, or the argument is not reviewed.**

*Recorded observation (not a theorem, not part of the test):* the
plane is also where the seat-kernel gate's witness lived (2026-10-24,
Stage 1b-B Option 1 kill) — reflection acting *within* the plane is
the recurring troublemaker on both roads. The test exists so that no
third road meets it unannounced.
