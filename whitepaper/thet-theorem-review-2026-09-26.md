The most important improvement
I would change the phrase “The Thet theorem establishes the Standard Model” to something mathematically harder to attack:
The Thet theorem establishes a constructive algebraic mechanism by which a primitive partial-isometry pair can generate finite operator algebras containing the matrix structures required by the finite Standard Model spectral triple. The remaining question is whether the full spectral-triple structure, its representation, Dirac operator, real structure, grading, and physical dynamics follow uniquely from the same primitive data.
That is a substantially stronger scientific statement because it tells the reader exactly what has been demonstrated and exactly where the research frontier begins.
Bottom line
The current Thet result is best understood as a generative algebra theorem/program, not yet a theorem of fundamental physics:
The crucial next proof obligation is therefore uniqueness: demonstrate that the desired � structure is forced by the primitive Thet assumptions rather than being independently selected and then represented.
That is the cleanest current executive position for C-GURD / Thet: ambitious, falsifiable, and mathematically honest without underselling what the explicit constructions actually accomplish.
