Abstract

This paper states exactly what the machine has verified in the Active Cartesian Modular Dynamics (ACMD) program through Pitch 4, Stage 1b, together with the SM-Match program and the first round of the theories intake. Every theorem named here is proved in Lean 4 (tier T1), explicitly axiomatized with proved consequences (T2), stated as a labeled open target (T3/T5), or recorded as refuted. The program is machine-verified mathematical scaffolding toward a rigorous four-dimensional quantum field theory: a finite spectral triple and its classification; the Connes–Kreimer Hopf algebra of renormalization; the almost-commutative product with its twist derived rather than assumed; analytic and Hilbert-space infrastructure for a concrete model; verdicts on all six Wightman axioms in a one-mode fermionic model; genuine Schwartz test-function machinery and a geometric Poincaré action; a finite-dimensional Stone theorem and a joint functional calculus for commuting symmetric operators; a Fock-space inner product with proved creation/annihilation adjoints on both the fermionic and bosonic sides; fermionic second quantization — the functorial lift Γ(U), the differential lift dΓ, and a free Hamiltonian whose gap is derived at finite modes; the bosonic dΓ lift with its own derived free gap, and the bosonic Γ(U) lift, completed as a verified functor by the same-day campaign that proved the two permanent identities its functoriality had been priced at; a structural match against the Standard Model's gauge algebra, fermion content, classical Lagrangian sectors, and Higgs breaking pattern; and fit verdicts for KMS theory, classical Yang–Mills, Haag–Kastler algebraic QFT, and the finite spectral action. Version 3 adds: the free theory at countably many modes for both statistics, with the derived free gap in the eigenvalue-on-finite-particle-domain sense (Pitch 2); interacting Hamiltonians at finite modes — the fermionic campaign stands whole with gap persistence, and the bosonic campaign's sole priced clause, essential self-adjointness, was discharged the following day (Pitch 3 stands whole; §0 carries the correction to Version 3's conditional verdict, in the open); unitary Schrödinger dynamics with picture equivalence and proved time-invariance of the interacting gap clusters; full restricted-Lorentz invariance of the free one-particle mass-shell measure (the Rung 7 boost window, discharged); and the opening of the lattice road toward the continuum (Pitch 4): Wilson-action lattice gauge theory with exact gauge invariance, the Gibbs measure and correlation package, Elitzur's theorem at a general gauge group and every coupling, the reflection structure, the Osterwalder–Schrader form with positivity proved at zero coupling and reduced at general coupling to one named lemma, the OS Hilbert space, and the transfer matrix — with the lattice gap proved exactly at zero coupling and fenced, with its price, at general coupling. Version 4 adds: the first mixed fermion–boson (Yukawa-shape) interaction, with windowed gap persistence (§18); the thermal clock — every faithful finite state carries its modular clock, and for Gibbs states physical time is β × thermal time (§19); the complete screening of the XYZ triadic sector grid — all 24 named triples killed and the sector existence statement refuted, with Open Problem 2 itself untouched and open (§20); Pitch 4 Stage 2 (one exact blocking step; the qualitative constraint set proved closed under it; no quantitative uniform bound) and Stage 1c-0, PARTIAL (§21); strong continuity of the one-particle representation for both subgroups and its Fock-level packaging and Lorentz transport (§22); the Schrödinger exponential and Duhamel remainders and the Bridge exhibit's spectral completion (§23); the corpus's first partial trace, the pre-compensation reachability theorem, and the resonance-spectrum pin (§24); the closed Round 2 intake — the Osterwalder–Schrader re-pin, the Dixmier-trace intake (whose finite evaluation is identically zero, against the intake's own prediction), and the finite BRST intake (§25); the discharge of all three co-import fences, so the whole corpus imports into one environment (§26); the Wightman re-pin on genuine tempered distributions, with the old existence statements — the 2026 pin's and Rung 4's sharpened bundle alike — proved vacuous and replaced by failable ones (§27); and two exhibits of a different kind: a pre-registered experiment that killed its own direction (the trit experiment, §28) and a numerical portrait of the Stage 0/1a vacuum (§28).

What this is not. No continuum limit is claimed. No interacting 4D quantum field theory is claimed. Interacting Hamiltonians are constructed (§10B) — at finite modes, for matter self-interaction only, with no gauge field; no interacting continuum theory of either statistics is constructed, and no scattering content appears anywhere. The Yang–Mills existence and mass gap problem is the program's stated horizon and mission target; it is not claimed, approached in the Clay sense, or implied by anything below. The mass gap appears in this paper only as a fixed, quarantined target statement against which future partial results can be measured; the gaps that are derived below (§§9, 10, 10A, and the lattice gap of §13A) are gaps of free Hamiltonians at finite or countably many modes, of an interacting finite-mode Hamiltonian under a proved persistence threshold, and of a finite-lattice transfer matrix at zero coupling — labeled as such wherever they occur.
