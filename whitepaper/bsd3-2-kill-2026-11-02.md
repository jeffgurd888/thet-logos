## Root-cause analysis (recorded for the parent's ruling — NOT adopted)

for curves with **a₃ = 0**. The general criterion uses
`(2y + a₁x + a₃)² ∣ Δ`. For E3 (a₁ = 0, a₃ = 1) the correct
quantity is (2y+1)², and it **does** divide Δ at all four
points (121, 121, 14641, 14641) — matching the study's own
§2 consistency note. E1 and E2 never stressed the distinction:
E1's torsion all has y = 0; E2's torsion is trivial. **BSD3-2
did its chartered job — it chose a curve stressing a seam the
earlier curves leave idle, and the seam broke.**

**Scope of the damage, stated exactly:** the E1 and E2 seam

(b) any future reuse of the `yi² ∣ Δ` form for a curve with
a₃ ≠ 0. The landed BSD3-2 PIN stands as a pin — it proves
nothing, so nothing proved falls — but its hypothesis list
