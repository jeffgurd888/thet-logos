## What this brick is

FG-2c-3 is Route B, the Yosida construct. It takes the resolvent
family discharged by FG-2c-1 and the Hamiltonian discharged by
FG-2b and produces, from those objects alone, a one-parameter
family of contractions that behaves as the semigroup generated by
the Hamiltonian. It is a construction, not an identification: what
is proved is that a semigroup with the required properties exists

## Brick 0 — the Hille–Yosida power bound and accretivity

Two items are discharged, the first of them a debt carried from
FG-2c-1:

  `‖(λ • R_λ)^k‖ ≤ 1` for all `k`, at every `λ > 0`. The bound

  FG-2c-1 debt is discharged in full.

## Brick 2 — the approximant semigroups and their limit

For every `n`, the approximant semigroup `exp(−tĤₙ)` is a
contraction. The Duhamel bound controls the difference between
approximants, and the approximants are Cauchy in `n` for every
`u ∈ H′`. Their limit `S(t)` is a continuous linear map on all
of `H′`, with `‖S(t)‖ ≤ 1`.

The pre-registered partial exit for this brick — a limit existing
only on the domain of `Ĥ` — did not occur. The limit is on the
whole carrier.
