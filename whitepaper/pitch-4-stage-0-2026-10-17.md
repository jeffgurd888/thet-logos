## Scoped vs landed, per brief item

1. **Gauge action; plaquettes conjugate; trace/Re tr invariant —
   STANDS.** `gaugeAction (U^g)(x,μ) = g x · U(x,μ) · (g (σ μ x))⁻¹`;
   `plaquette_gauge`: `U_p(U^g) = g x · U_p(U) · (g x)⁻¹` (the proof
   uses shift commutation `σ ν (σ μ x) = σ μ (σ ν x)` — with this
   gauge-action convention the two walks around the square must agree);
   `trace_repMatrix_conj` gives trace invariance, hence density and
   action invariance.
2. **Exact gauge invariance of the Wilson action for all `g` —
   STANDS (the stage headline).** `wilsonAction_gauge_invariant :
   S_β(U^g) = S_β(U)` for **all** `g : Λ → G`, no gauge fixing anywhere
   (pre-kill (a) honored by construction).
3. **Measure invariance — STANDS in the strong form.** The scoped
   fallback (integral invariance only) was *not* needed:
   `configMeasure_map_gaugeAction` proves
   `Measure.map (gaugeAction σ g) (configMeasure μG) = configMeasure μG`,
   via Mathlib's product-measure preservation under per-coordinate
   measure-preserving maps (each coordinate map is a left/right
   translation pair). `integral_gaugeAction` is the integral corollary.
   Right-invariance and inversion-invariance of the normalized Haar
   measure itself are proved in-file (`Measure.IsMulRightInvariant
   (haarProb G)`, `Measure.IsInvInvariant (haarProb G)`) from
   uniqueness of normalized Haar measure on a compact group.
4. **Wilson loops — STANDS.** Paths are lists of `Step`s (`fwd μ` /
   `bwd μ` from a base site); `wilsonLoop = tr ρ(loopProd)`;
   `wilsonLoop_gauge_invariant` for closed loops by conjugation plus
   trace cyclicity (`trace_repMatrix_conj`). The plaquette is a closed
   loop in the formalism: `loopProd_plaquetteSteps` (uses commutation).
   Expectations `⟨W_C⟩_β` defined (`wilsonLoopExpectation` via
   `gibbsExpect`).
5. **β = 0 package — STANDS, in general `(G, ρ)` form (stronger than
   the scoped floor).** `gibbsExpect_zero`: at `β = 0` the Gibbs
   expectation is the plain product-Haar expectation. The Haar
   intertwiner `P = ∫ ρ dμ` is analyzed directly: intertwining on both
   sides (`repMatrix_mul_haarIntertwiner`, `haarIntertwiner_mul_repMatrix`),
   idempotence (`haarIntertwiner_idempotent`), range = fixed vectors
   (`repMatrix_mulVec_haarIntertwiner`, `haarIntertwiner_mulVec_of_fixed`),
   and `P = 0` when `ρ` has no nonzero fixed vectors
   (`haarIntertwiner_eq_zero_of_no_fixed`). Single-link expectations
   factor through `P` (`integral_repMatrix_link`,
   `integral_trace_repMatrix_link`), and the plaquette expectation
   factorizes over its four distinct links into `P⁴ = P`:
   **`gibbsExpect_zero_plaquette : ⟨W_p⟩₀ = tr P`** and
   **`gibbsExpect_zero_plaquette_eq_zero : ⟨W_p⟩₀ = 0`** when `ρ` has
   no nonzero fixed vectors — also in Wilson-loop form
   (`gibbsExpect_zero_wilsonLoop_plaquette`). This is Haar
   orthogonality in projection form; no shaped-instance partial was
   needed, and nothing is priced here. The factorization needs only
   `σ μ x ≠ x`, `σ ν x ≠ x`, `μ ≠ ν` (the four plaquette links are then
   pairwise distinct) — commutation is *not* needed for the β = 0
   plaquette theorem. **Instances discharged (§8):** the circle's
   fundamental representation (`circle_plaquette_expectation_zero`)
   and the unitary group's identity representation at every `d`
   (`unitary_plaquette_expectation_zero`; `d = 2` is the SU(2)-shaped
   case up to the determinant condition, which these expectations do
   not see) — both have no nonzero fixed vectors (witnesses: `z = −1`;
   a diagonal sign flip), so both get `⟨W_p⟩₀ = 0` on the torus for
   `N ≥ 2`.
6. **Finite identities layer — STANDS whole.** `plaquette_one`
   (trivial configuration), `plaquette_swap`
   (`U_p(x,ν,μ) = U_p(x,μ,ν)⁻¹`), the unitary trace bounds
   `re_trace_le_of_mem_unitaryGroup` / `neg_re_trace_le_…` from the
   entry bound `abs_entry_of_mem_unitaryGroup` (entries of a unitary
   matrix have norm ≤ 1), the density bound `plaquetteDensity_mem_Icc`
   (density ∈ [0, 2] for `0 < d`), action nonnegativity
   `wilsonAction_nonneg` and the explicit bound `wilsonAction_le`
   for `β ≥ 0`, and denominator positivity `gibbsDenom_pos`
   (`β ≥ 0`), which makes the ratio-of-integrals expectation honest.

## Design choices (delegated by §6.1, disclosed)

- **Lattice presentation:** abstract finite site type `Λ [Fintype Λ]`
  with four shift bijections `σ : Fin 4 → Equiv.Perm Λ`; commutation
  is a *hypothesis* on exactly the theorems that use it
  (`plaquette_gauge`, the loop identifications), never baked into
  definitions. The concrete torus `Fin 4 → ZMod N` (shifts =
  `Equiv.addLeft (Pi.single μ 1)`) is instantiated in §8:
  `torusShift_comm`, and `torusShift_ne_self` for `2 ≤ N` — the
  abstract layer is inhabited by the intended model.
- **Representation device:** a general monoid homomorphism
  `ρ : G →* ↥(Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin d) ℂ)` (the brief's subgroup
  device, stated at its natural generality: the unitary-group instance
  is the identity homomorphism). `repMatrix ρ g := (ρ g).1`.
- **Plaquette convention:**
  `U_p(x,μ,ν) = U(x,μ) · U(σ μ x, ν) · (U(σ ν x, μ))⁻¹ · (U(x,ν))⁻¹`;
  the action sums over `x`, `μ`, and `ν ∈ Ioi μ` (each unordered
  plaquette once).
- **Measure device:** `haarProb G := ((haar univ)⁻¹) • haar`, built
  in-file from Mathlib's `Measure.haar` by scalar normalization (a
  ready-made normalized-Haar-probability instance under that name was
  not available in the pinned Mathlib; its Haar/right/inversion
  invariance is proved in-file from uniqueness). All measure theorems
  are parametrized over any `μG` with `[IsProbabilityMeasure μG]
  [IsMulLeftInvariant μG] [IsMulRightInvariant μG] [IsInvInvariant μG]`.
- **Expectation device:** `gibbsExpect` = ratio of integrals
  `∫ e^{−S} F dμ / ∫ e^{−S} dμ` (the brief's explicitly allowed
  device; no Gibbs measure object is constructed).

## Deviations and accommodations (all disclosed in the module header)

- **`[SecondCountableTopology G]`** is carried as a hypothesis in the
  integration sections (§6–§7) — in this Mathlib it is what makes
  multiplication of `G`-valued measurable functions measurable. Both
  instance families satisfy it (for the unitary group it is supplied
  in-module, see below).
- **The intertwiner is defined entrywise** (`Matrix.of fun i j => ∫ g,
  repMatrix ρ g i j ∂μG`): Mathlib deliberately registers no global
  norm on matrices, so the matrix-valued Bochner integral is not
  available off the shelf; the entrywise integral is the same object.
- **In-module topological instances for `↥(Matrix.unitaryGroup (Fin d)
  ℂ)`** (§8): Mathlib provides the group structure but not
  `ContinuousMul`, `IsTopologicalGroup`, or `CompactSpace` for it;
  all three are proved in-module (continuity of inversion via
  `⇑A⁻¹ = star A`; compactness as a closed subset of a product of
  entry disks, using the entry bound). The Pi-transported
  `LocallyCompactSpace` / `SecondCountableTopology` instances on
  matrices themselves are also registered in-module (Pi type with the
  Pi topology), and second countability of the subtype goes through
  `TopologicalSpace.secondCountableTopology_induced`.
- **`repMatrix_inv` is stated in star form** (`repMatrix ρ g⁻¹ =
  star (repMatrix ρ g)`), the form the unitary-group API gives
  directly.
- The module is **not in the root import list** (corpus pattern for
  recent exhibits); verified by targeted build.
