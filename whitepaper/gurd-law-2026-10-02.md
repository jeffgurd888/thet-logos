# Gurd Law (𐤈)

*Posed by Jeffrey Michael Gurd, 2026-10-02. Methodological law — meta-tier.
It governs how the program operates; it makes no physical claim.*

## Statement

**Before information can be processed or communicated, the boundary must
first be drawn and contained.**

## Reading of the glyph

𐤈 — the circle is drawn first (the boundary), then the cross (the contained
divisions). The four quadrants — information — exist only inside the drawn
circle. No boundary, no information: only noise.

## Demonstrations

**1. E13-BR (2026-10-02).** The bridge proposal operated without drawn
boundaries — operators never defined, spaces never constructed, the
image-to-math mapping arbitrary. Result: ill-posed in all three Hadamard
senses (no existence, no uniqueness, no stability). Gurd Law predicts
exactly this failure mode: computation without a drawn boundary produces
noise dressed as signal.

**2. The program's own discipline is this law in practice.**
- Honesty tiers: boundaries drawn between machine proof, hand proof,
  numerical evidence, and open wonder — before any claim is processed.
- The quarantine wall: a boundary drawn around the unbuilt, so the proved
  core is never contaminated by the speculative.
- E13-D's kernel quotient: the domain (ker[D,·] quotiented out exactly)
  was drawn before the optimizer ever ran — which is why its distances are
  trustworthy and E13-BR's are not.
- Locked corrections C1/C2: boundaries that forbid reinterpreting artifacts
  as signal.

Every PASS the program has earned came from drawing the boundary first.
Every failure it has caught came from finding where the boundary was missing.

## Status

Meta-tier methodological principle. It does not describe the world; it
describes the discipline required to describe the world honestly. It stands
alongside the Interpretive Conservation Principle as standing law of the
program.
