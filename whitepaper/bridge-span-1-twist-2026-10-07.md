## What this span is

The canyon: the Euclidean finite spectral triple vs. anything Lorentzian,
causal, or continuum. Bridge Span 1 is the **twisted-triple pylon** — the
Connes–Moscovici twisted-triple program with a twist-from-flow
`ρ_s(a) = e^{isK} a e^{−isK}`, `K = −ln ρ` synthesized from a state (the
seed-2026 lab construction). The question put to the pylon: does the
modular-flow twist carry the finite triple toward the Lorentzian side, in
the Devastato–Lizzi–Martinetti sense, or does it break the triple's own
axioms first? Answer (T4): **it breaks them first** — for this generic `K`.

- Inverse: `ρ_s⁻¹ = σ(K, −s, ·)` (group law, exact).

### The literature framework being tested (Connes–Moscovici / DLM)

A twisted triple carries: twist `ρ ∈ Aut(A)`; **regularity**
`ρ(a†) = (ρ⁻¹(a))†`; **twisted commutator** `[D,a]_ρ := Da − ρ(a)D` (bounded
required — automatic here); real-structure compatibility `ρ(J) = εJ`;
**twisted first-order** `[[D,a]_ρ, b°]_{ρ°} = 0` with
`ρ°(a°) := (ρ⁻¹(a))°`; **twisted 1-forms**
`Ω¹_D(A,ρ) = {Σ a_k[D,b_k]_ρ}` with the bimodule law
`a·A_ρ·b = ρ(a)A_ρ b`; **twisted fluctuation**
`D_{A_ρ} := D + A_ρ + ε₁ J A_ρ J⁻¹`; **twisted gauge** `D → ρ(U) D U†`.

DLM (Devastato–Lizzi–Martinetti): the minimal twist by grading (flip on
`A ⊗ ℂ²`) links the twist to **Lorentzian signature via the ρ-product
(Krein)**; the twisted fluctuation can drive Euclidean→Lorentzian in the
fermionic action. Connes–Moscovici's original motivation: the twist is
related to the **modular automorphism group** (type III). Our move: replace
the abstract twist with twist-from-flow — the one-parameter inner group
generated by the synthesized modular Hamiltonian.

**What the lab does not test:** the ε, ε₁, ε″ sign table; the full
twisted-spectral-action; any Krein/ρ-product construction. Those are
unbuilt, not refuted.

### Reading the verdicts

- **A.1 (regularity):** the Connes–Moscovici identity
  `ρ(a†) = (ρ⁻¹(a))†` fails linearly in `s` (residual `~ 2|s|‖[K,a†]‖`).
  What holds is the weaker property: unitary conjugation is a
  `*`-automorphism (`ρ_s(a†) = ρ_s(a)†`, residual exactly 0). **Lesson:
  involutive twists (`ρ² = id`), not one-parameter inner groups, are the
  natural carriers of CM regularity.** This is the same structural lesson
  the relabeling rule rewards: the strong claim dies, the weaker exact
  property stands.
- **A.2 (preservation):** the generic `K` **mixes the 16-sector with the
  dead sector** — `ρ_s ∉ Aut(π(A_F))` for every `s ≠ 0` tested; by
  `|s| = 2` the residual saturates at the full norm of the generators
  themselves. The twist does not stay inside the represented algebra.
  (s = 0 residual ~1e−16 is trivial.)
- **A.3 (J-compatibility):** `ρ_s(J) = εJ` cannot hold with this `K` —
  `[K, U_J] ≠ 0` at relative scale 0.56.
- **A.4 (commutators):** the twisted commutator is **not** a tamer
  commutator. It is 12× larger at `|s| = 0.1` and ~55× at `|s| = 1` than the
  untwisted one. Whatever regularity the twist buys in the literature, it
  does not buy it here by shrinking commutators.
- **A.5 (the s→0 limit):** clean and linear → 0, exactly as the algebraic
  identity `[D,a]_{ρ_s} − [D,a] = (π(a) − σ_s(π(a)))D` predicts. **This is
  the one behavior the twist gets right** — continuity at the identity,
  T4-consistent with the T1-track recovery theorems (`twistedComm_twistConj_one`,
  `twistedComm_flow_zero`, `modularTwist_zero`, §(e) T1-2).
- **B (twisted order-one):** s = 0 reproduces the 576-pair census
  (0.000e+00 — the machine-checked order-one condition is untouched).
  For `s ≠ 0` the twisted first-order condition fails with max residual
  **53.31** at s = 0.5 and **59.39** at s = 1.0 (36 of 576 pairs nonzero).
  **The twist breaks order-one badly.** This is the pylon's central load
  test, and the pylon fails it.
- **C (fluctuation):** `D_{A_ρ} = D + A_ρ + J A_ρ J⁻¹` is **not**
  self-adjoint for twisted 1-forms — neither for a random 3-term
  `A_ρ` (residual 2.92e+04) nor for one built from the self-adjoint
  represented basis (residual 36.1; note the basis itself is only
  2-dimensional — the Option-A artifact again). A **twist-corrected
  reality prescription** is needed before any twisted fluctuation can be
  physical; flagged, beyond this span's scope.

## (c) The Bisognano–Wichmann analog, posed precisely **(T5 — vision, obstructed)**

### What BW actually says (cited honestly)

In relativistic quantum field theory satisfying the standard axioms
(Wightman / Haag–Kastler), for a **wedge region**
`W = {x : x¹ > |x⁰|}` the Tomita–Takesaki modular group of the vacuum
state restricted to the wedge algebra `M(W)` acts **geometrically**:
`σ_t^Ω(A) = U(Λ(−2πt)) A U(Λ(−2πt))⁻¹` — the modular flow *is* the Lorentz
boost subgroup (Bisognano–Wichmann, 1975–76). This is the theorem our
bridge idea echoes: Connes–Moscovici's original motivation ties the
twist to the **modular automorphism group** (type III), and our twist
`ρ_s = Ad(e^{isK})` is a finite-dimensional echo of a modular flow. A
finite BW analog would therefore claim: *the flow acts geometrically on a
subalgebra playing the wedge role.*

### The obstruction (labeled **BW-OBSTRUCTION**, proposed catalog entry)

A finite BW analog would require, at minimum:

1. **A wedge-role subalgebra:** a proper subalgebra `A_W ⊂ π(A_F)` with
   wedge-like structure — nontrivial relative commutant, a locality
   assignment `region ↦ algebra`. **We have none.** `π(A_F)` is a
   6-complex-dimensional represented algebra with no localization
   structure, no position operators, no spacetime regions.
2. **A geometric action:** the flow `σ_s` normalizing `A_W` and acting on
   it in a way indexed by wedge-like data (the analog of
   `Λ(−2πt)` moving the wedge). **We have none.** The flow acts by unitary
   conjugation — **isospectral, compact, quasi-periodic** eigenspace
   rotation. Boosts are non-compact hyperbolic. The modular-flow lab
   (`modular-flow-lab-2026-10-07.md`) explicitly checked and found **no
   boost signature**; the BW moral survives only in miniature (the flow
   moves the *representation* — eigenspaces, commutators, distances —
   while the spectrum stands still).
3. **Spacetime to be geometric in:** a causal structure, a Poincaré
   representation, points. **We have none** — `H = ℂ³²`.

**Verdict: the finite BW analog is QUARANTINED.** It is proposed as a
catalog §2 open-hypothesis entry (weak, precise restatement allowed) or a
§3 K-list entry (blocked-by-obstruction); per the §4 relabeling rule,
reopening requires new evidence stated in the open — specifically, a
localization structure on the finite triple, which does not exist today.
The BW analog joins the K2-quarantine family (Tet→SM bridge / continuum /
cosmology): no wedges, no spacetime, no localization, no claim.

### The pylon verdict: dead and live, pinned

**DEAD (this generic K, seed 2026):** twist-from-flow as a Connes–Moscovici
twisted triple. It fails CM regularity (A.1), fails algebra preservation
(A.2), fails J-compatibility (A.3), inflates the twisted commutator
(A.4), breaks twisted order-one (B), and its fluctuation needs a
reality prescription that doesn't exist yet (C). The BW analog is
quarantined (c). Per the relabeling rule, these verdicts stand; the
claims return only as new, weaker entries by proof.

**LIVE options, named honestly:**

1. **Involutive twist** (`ρ² = id`): the A.1 lesson. CM regularity wants
   involution, not a one-parameter inner group. DLM's minimal twist by
   grading (flip on `A ⊗ ℂ²`) is an involutive candidate from the
   literature — **untested here**, not claimed.

   drawn) to normalize `π(A_F)` and commute with `U_J`. The A.1–A.3
   refutations are scoped to the generic seed-2026 `K` and do not cover
   this. Open, unbuilt.
3. **Flow-orbit algebra:** drop the twisted-triple program and study the
   algebra generated by `{σ_s(a) : s ∈ ℝ, a ∈ A_F}` — a different object
   with its own ledger. The modular-flow lab's kernel-destruction finding
   (the flow moves `ker[D,·]` out of `π(A_F)`) is the first entry in that
